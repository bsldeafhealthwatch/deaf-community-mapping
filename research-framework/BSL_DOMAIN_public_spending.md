# Domain 11: Public Spending & Contracts (Interpreting Agency Spend, ICB Commissioning, Procurement)

**Last verified:** 4 September 2026
**Re-check cadence:** On any new NHS/government interpreting framework award
**Confidence level:** Medium for framework values and dates (Contracts Finder/Find a Tender are primary procurement sources); Low for any BSL-specific spend breakdown, which does not appear to exist anywhere in public procurement data

---

## Summary

This domain maps the actual contracts and frameworks through which BSL interpreting services are purchased by the public sector, as a companion to Domain 8's regulatory picture and Domain 12's waste-vs-grassroots lens. The headline structural finding: **large national interpreting frameworks bundle spoken-language and BSL/non-spoken interpreting together**, and no public body appears to publish a BSL-only spend total. The NHS SBS framework (£73m, with a dedicated BSL lot) is the most BSL-specific procurement vehicle found; the much larger NHS PiP Language Services framework (£400m ceiling) covers BSL only as a minority lot within a mostly-spoken-language vehicle. This bundling is itself a finding relevant to Domain 12 (Waste vs. grassroots) — it is structurally difficult for the public or campaigners to isolate what proportion of public money is actually reaching BSL interpretation specifically.

## Key Organisations & Actors

- **NHS SBS (Shared Business Services)** — runs the £73m framework with the dedicated BSL lot, the most directly relevant procurement vehicle for this project's health-access focus
- **NHS PiP (Procurement in Partnership, Wales-hosted but England-facing)** — runs the much larger £400m language-services ceiling framework, of which BSL is a minority component
- **DA Languages (Dals)** — the commercial supplier appearing most frequently across multiple separate award notices found in this pass (NHS SBS, UKHSA, Leicestershire Police, Portsmouth/IoW NHS) — a candidate for closer scrutiny given its recurring presence across public-sector language contracts generally, not BSL specifically
- **Deaf Cultural Centre** — notable as a Deaf-sector-specific (rather than generic commercial language-agency) supplier listed on the NHS SBS framework's BSL lot
- **Sign Language Interactions, Sign Solutions, thebigword** — other suppliers named on the PiP framework's non-spoken lots

## Current Policy / Legal Position

No dedicated statutory procurement requirement specific to BSL interpreting was found — public bodies procure BSL interpreting through generic language-services frameworks (NHS SBS, NHS PiP) governed by standard public procurement law (the Procurement Act 2023 regime, not independently reviewed in this pass), with BSL treated as one lot among many rather than a standalone procurement category with its own rules. This is consistent with Domain 8's finding that acceptable-register requirements (NRCPD/RBSLI/SRLPDC) are set per-framework rather than by overarching statute.

## Known Gaps & Contested Figures

- **No published national BSL-only spend total exists at any level** — not NHS-wide, not government-wide. Every located figure is either a whole-framework ceiling covering many languages (£400m, £73m) or a single supplier's award value for a multi-language contract. This is the central, load-bearing gap in this domain and should be treated as a headline finding for the project (paralleling Domain 5's "no BSL-only AtW spend figure" and Domain 4's "no disaggregated PIP data" findings) — there is a consistent pattern across this entire research framework of public bodies not disaggregating BSL-specific spend or outcomes from broader categories.
- DA Languages' recurring appearance across multiple, separately-sourced contract award notices in this pass may simply reflect it being a large, generalist language-services provider that wins many public contracts — this is not evidence of anything improper on its own, and should not be framed that way without further investigation; it is flagged here only because its frequency makes it a natural next FOI/scrutiny target.
- The NHS SBS framework's supplier list (34 suppliers) has not been cross-checked against NRCPD/RBSLI registration status per Domain 8's procurement-standards question — worth doing directly.

## Money Trail

- £73m (NHS SBS, BSL-inclusive) + £400m ceiling (NHS PiP, BSL-minority) = at least £473m in national NHS-facing framework capacity that BSL interpreting sits somewhere within, with no way from public data to isolate what share BSL actually receives
- This bundling problem is the direct evidentiary gap Domain 12 (Waste vs. grassroots) needs closed before it can make any specific claim about BSL interpreting spend efficiency — without a BSL-only baseline figure, there is no denominator against which to assess whether spend is reaching frontline interpretation or being absorbed elsewhere
