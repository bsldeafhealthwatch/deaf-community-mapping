# Domain 6: Neurodivergence Overlap (DeafDisabled, Deaf+ Dual/Multiple Needs)

**Last verified:** 4 September 2026
**Re-check cadence:** Quarterly
**Confidence level:** Low — this is the thinnest domain in the project so far; almost nothing found traces to a primary, UK, Deaf-and-ND-specific source

---

## Summary

This domain asks how many Deaf BSL users are also autistic, have ADHD, or have another neurodivergent or additional need, and how well services serve that intersection. The honest answer from this pass is: **there is no reliable UK prevalence figure for neurodivergent Deaf BSL users**, and the closest official-adjacent data point (CRIDE's "additional needs" figure for deaf children) measures a different, broader category. What does exist is qualitative evidence of a service gap — inaccessible autism/ADHD/dementia assessment pathways, a near-absence of BSL-informed neuropsychological assessment tools, and no dedicated UK national statutory service for the Deaf-neurodivergent intersection. This is one of the least-evidenced domains in the whole framework and should be flagged as a research priority rather than a source of quotable figures.

## Current Policy / Legal Position

No dedicated legislation or national policy specific to Deaf-neurodivergent dual needs was found in this pass. This population's rights would run through the same general frameworks already documented elsewhere in this project — the Equality Act 2010 (reasonable adjustments), the Children and Families Act 2014/SEND framework for children with EHCPs (see Domain 15, Education), and NHS autism/ADHD assessment pathways generally, none of which have a BSL-specific adaptation requirement written into policy at a national level.

---

## Known Gaps & Contested Figures

- **No prevalence figure exists for neurodivergent Deaf BSL users of any age** — this is the single clearest gap in this domain and arguably in the whole project. The CRIDE "additional needs" figure is the nearest available proxy but should never be presented as an autism/ADHD prevalence figure; it measures a broader, differently-defined category (deaf children with *any* additional need, not specifically neurodivergence, and not adults).
- No Deaf-specific breakout exists within NHS England's autism assessment waiting-list statistics (236,225 open referrals, 211,104 waiting 13+ weeks) — meaning it's currently impossible to know how long Deaf autistic people specifically wait, or whether BSL-accessible assessment is available at all within that queue.
- The claim that Dr Atkinson's BSL-based assessment tools are not standard NHS provision is an inference from her being individually named as having developed them, not a directly documented policy statement — worth confirming directly (e.g., via NHS England or UCL DCAL, her likely institutional base) before treating as established.
- No dedicated national organisation or programme for the Deaf-neurodivergent intersection was found — this itself may be a useful, publishable finding (a genuine service gap), but should be double-checked against BDA and NDCS directly, since they were not the primary sources checked in this pass.

## FOI Targets Arising

1. **NHS England** — whether autism/ADHD assessment waiting-list data can be disaggregated by communication need (BSL user status), even if not currently published
2. **UCL DCAL / relevant NHS trusts** — clarification on whether Dr Atkinson's BSL-based neuropsychological assessment tools are used anywhere in routine NHS practice, and if so, where and how accessibly
3. **BDA and NDCS directly** — whether either organisation holds or knows of any UK prevalence data or service mapping for Deaf-neurodivergent individuals not surfaced in this pass
4. **CRIDE/BATOD** — whether the "additional needs" category in the annual survey can be broken down further into neurodivergence-specific subcategories
