# Domain 2: Mental Health Services & Deaf/DeafBlind Access (incl. BSMHD)

**Last verified:** 4 September 2026
**Re-check cadence:** Quarterly, or on any change to SignHealth's NHS commissioning status
**Confidence level:** Low-Medium — the headline prevalence figures are a secondary citation inside an advisory report, not an independently verified study; the service-provision facts (SignHealth contract status) are more solid

---

## Summary

Deaf BSL users are reported to experience mental health difficulties at roughly twice the rate of the general population, with longer inpatient stays when admitted — a striking figure that should be traced to its original study before being used as a headline claim, since it currently only appears as a citation inside the BSL Advisory Board's *Locked Out* report rather than as a directly reviewed primary study. The practical service picture is more concrete and more concerning: SignHealth's national NHS Talking Therapies contract with NHS England **ended 31 March 2025**, moving funding onto a fragmented Integrated Care Board (ICB)-by-ICB basis — meaning continued access to specialist Deaf psychological therapy now depends on local commissioning decisions rather than a single national funding stream. Crisis pathways (111/999) have no published BSL-completion statistic, and only 7.65% of interpreters are reported willing to accept mental health work, directly constraining capacity even where funding exists.

## Key Organisations & Actors

- **SignHealth** — the central Deaf-led mental-health service provider in this domain, operating both the Psychological Therapy Service and (per Domain 13/10) InterpreterNow; its NHS England contract ending in Mar 2025 is the single most consequential recent development in this domain
- **NHS England** — held and then ended the national Talking Therapies contract with SignHealth; ICBs are now the relevant commissioning bodies
- **National High Secure Deaf Service (NHSDS, Rampton)** — specialist high-secure inpatient provision with a BSL-trained team, per BMJ reporting
- **SWLSTG National Deaf Services (Corner House + National Deaf CAMHS)** — Corner House was a Tier 4, nationally-commissioned specialist Deaf inpatient CAMHS service serving ages 11-17 UK-wide, but **its inpatient unit closed permanently on 1 July 2026** (confirmed 6 Sept 2026), following an NHS England review; funding moved to four new regional specialist community teams (South East, Central, North, South West). National Deaf CAMHS's outpatient/community strand continues (London/Kent/Cambridge, plus hearing children of Deaf adults). This is the second closure in this service's recent history — it also closed from around August 2022 to 1 June 2023 pending an earlier review, at which point it served a wider age range (8-18) than its later 11-17 configuration; the 2026 closure is stated as permanent, not a pending-review pause
- **BSL Advisory Board** — via *Locked Out*, the source of the prevalence figures and the crisis-pathway findings

## Current Policy / Legal Position

No BSL- or Deaf-specific statutory right to mental health provision exists beyond the general Equality Act 2010 reasonable-adjustments duty (the same recurring structural pattern found in Domains 1, 4, 7, and 13). The practically significant policy event in this domain is not legislative but commissioning-structural: the shift of SignHealth's core NHS-funded service from a single national NHS England contract to fragmented ICB-by-ICB commissioning as of 24 Feb/31 Mar 2025. This mirrors a wider NHS commissioning pattern (national specialised commissioning devolving to local ICBs) but has a specific, sharp consequence for a small, specialist national service like SignHealth's: continuity of care now depends on 42 individual ICBs each choosing to fund it, rather than one national decision.

## Known Gaps & Contested Figures

- **The 40% vs. 25% prevalence figure and "twice the length of inpatient admission" claim need their original source traced.** They currently appear only as a citation inside *Locked Out*, itself citing SignHealth — this is a second-hand citation of a citation, and per the framework's own verification discipline this should not be presented as a headline statistic until the original study is located and opened directly. This is flagged as the single highest-priority verification task in this domain.
- **No current published national bed census or waiting-time series exists for Deaf-specific mental health inpatient units** — the existence of NHSDS and SWLSTG National Deaf Services is documented, but capacity and wait-time data is not. This parallels the "no disaggregated data" pattern found across Domains 4, 5, and 11.
- **No official NHS statistic for BSL completion rates on 111/999 crisis contacts** — a clear, concrete FOI target given how specific and answerable this question is (NHS 111/999 providers should hold interpreter-usage logs).
- Whether ICB-by-ICB commissioning of SignHealth's therapy service has actually resulted in service loss/gaps in any specific ICB area since Feb/Mar 2025 was not investigated in this pass — this is the most actionable, checkable near-term fact this domain is missing, and directly relevant to the project's own SWL geographic footprint (worth checking which SWL-area ICB(s) have or haven't picked up funding).

## Money Trail

- SignHealth's financial position (Domain 10: income £9.736m, expenditure £10.397m, a deficit, with 94.6% of spend on charitable activities and £6.875m from 94 government contracts) directly contextualises this domain's central finding — a financially strained, contract-dependent charity just lost its single largest, most stable funding relationship (the national NHS England Talking Therapies contract) and must now rebuild funding ICB-by-ICB. This is a concrete, quantifiable organisational risk to Deaf-specific mental health provision in England.
