## Summary

This domain was split out deliberately from Domain 5 (Access to Work) and the national-DWP material now in that file's "National DWP Accessibility" section, because in-person Jobcentre Plus service delivery is a genuinely different evidence question from national DWP policy: a ministerial answer or a five-year strategic plan is evidence of *capability or intent*, not proof of what happens at a named local Jobcentre appointment. A Deaf BSL user must still ask before every Jobcentre appointment — there is no standing auto-booked interpreter and no published Jobcentre-level BSL fulfilment target. On-site Video Remote Interpreting was piloted at two offices (including Bromley) in December 2021; ministers describe VRS/VRI as available "in all Jobcentres," but DWP's own July 2025 BSL 5-year plan still treats meeting-level video communications as a two-year build, implying the desk-level rollout was not finished business as of that plan's publication. Work-coach Deaf-specific training does not exist as a named mandatory course — it is a scenario inside generic PSED induction. The two published in-person failure cases located are *Rimmer v SSWP* (Park Place, Leeds, 2017-2023, £49,880 tribunal award — both confirmed directly from the judgment) and the Clarke complaint (Surrey, DeafBlind/Usher syndrome, a BSL Level 3 colleague substituted for a registered interpreter — confirmed directly from the source article). None of the six South West London Jobcentres (Croydon, Kingston, Merton, Richmond, Sutton, Wandsworth) has a documented Jobcentre Plus partnership with local Deaf services, despite substantial local Deaf infrastructure existing in the same boroughs. Complaints use the ordinary DWP complaints ladder; interpreter-failure volumes are not published as a distinct category anywhere.

## Key Organisations & Actors

- **DWP / Jobcentre Plus** — operates the frontline in-person service this domain covers; publishes the (stale) IB reassessment factsheet and the current BSL 5-year plan
- **DA Languages Ltd ("Dals")** — current national language-services contractor (CCS RM6141 Lot 1); operates InterpretersLive! (formerly SignVideo-branded) VRS into DWP
- **thebigword** — historic F2F interpreting supplier at Park Place JCP (Leeds), 2017 to June 2022, per the Rimmer judgment
- **Employment Tribunal (Leeds)** — issued the *Rimmer v SSWP* judgment, this domain's central documented in-person failure case
- **Deaf Umbrella** — London/SE Deaf services provider; published the only located account of the Bromley JCP VRI pilot
- **ASLI (Association of Sign Language Interpreters)** — quoted rejecting "BSL Level 3 interpreter" as a valid qualification category in response to the Clarke case; also submitted written evidence on conditionality and training gaps in the Pathways to Work consultation
- **Sense** — submitted written evidence on assistive-technology gaps in Jobcentres and on DeafBlind claimants' experience more broadly
- **RAD (Royal Association for Deaf people), Kingston Interpreting Service, deafPLUS/Action Deafness, Oak Lodge School, Harding House (Achieve Together)** — named local Deaf/DeafBlind infrastructure in the SWL boroughs, none confirmed as a documented JCP partner

## Current Policy / Legal Position

Jobcentre Plus sits under the same legal frame as national DWP policy (Domain 5): Equality Act 2010 ss.20-21 and s.149 PSED, Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations 2018, and the BSL Act 2022's reporting duty — not the NHS/social-care Accessible Information Standard. The distinguishing feature of this domain is that a national policy document, PQ answer, or five-year plan is evidence of policy or capability only; it becomes evidence of local practice only when a source explicitly names the office, the booking, or the kit in question. This project's own reminder for this research batch flagged exactly this risk in advance, and it is treated as a standing rule for this domain: do not cite a ministerial "all Jobcentres" answer as proof that a specific SWL office has working on-site VRI.

## Known Gaps & Contested Figures

- **No published Jobcentre-level BSL interpreter fulfilment rate exists** (booked vs. present vs. assessment/appointment proceeding anyway). This is FOI-only territory.
- **No national on-site VRI inventory exists** — which offices have a working device, platform, and operating hours is not published anywhere located in this pass. The Bromley pilot (Dec 2021) is the only named, dated, sourced on-site VRI reference found; its outcome and whether it was extended nationally is not documented.
- **DeafBlind premises standard does not exist as a named DWP specification.** Loops and ramps are documented; tactile-signing space, low-visual-noise rooms, and guide-on-arrival provision for DeafBlind claimants specifically are not.
- **No official FTA/sanction count exists coded to "interpreter did not arrive" or "Level 3 colleague substituted."** Good-reason guidance (ADM K2) does not list interpreter failure as an example, so this is inferred from case evidence (Rimmer) rather than a published series.
- **The 635-Jobcentre assistive-technology figure was not independently re-located this session** and should be checked directly against the specific PQ cited (2231, Churchill, 24 Nov 2023) before being treated as confirmed — the only technology figure independently opened this pass (Sense's written evidence) cites a different total (639 Jobcentres) for a related but not identical claim.
- **None of the six SWL borough partnership claims were independently re-searched this session** — they are carried forward from the originating research pass. Given the specific, falsifiable nature of "no documented partnership," these are lower-risk negative findings than a positive statistic would be, but a genuine confirmation would mean directly checking each borough's own Jobcentre-specific pages (not just the council/charity pages already checked) before this is presented as settled.
