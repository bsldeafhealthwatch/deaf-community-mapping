The grid

Filter by design. Home rows are England, Scotland, Wales. Open a system for the five cells.

1 Duty

Equality Act reasonable adjustments + AIS. BSL Act 2022 is recognition/reporting only — no individual service right.

2 Payer

NHS body / ICB / GP contract. AtW pays work interpreting (cap £69,260 frozen). No isolated national BSL spend.

3 Booker

Clinic via a prime. SWL GP: ICB holds DAL from Oct 2024; Sign Solutions VRI sub. Hospitals often LanguageLine packages.

4 VRS

Not any-number. 999 BSL + 111 BSL. Else per-organisation contracts. Ofcom rejected unrestricted VRS in 2011.

5 Deafblind

RIDB 19 national / London 1. DAL and LanguageLine subcontract to Deafblind Enablement. No fulfilment series.

Steal nothing from the chain. Publish fulfilment next to the 878 requests. Duty exists; arrival is the missing column.

1 Duty

BSL (Scotland) Act 2015 requires plans — stronger than the UK 2022 Act. Boards still owe an interpreter under equality law.

2 Payer

Scottish Government funds Contact Scotland BSL (£3m ex VAT to Sign Solutions from 1 Dec 2025). F2F is a board pot.

3 Booker

User can place the phone call. Face-to-face remains the board’s booking.

4 VRS

Any UK number, 24/7, for residents. Not international or work calls. 60% of consultation respondents never used it.

5 Deafblind

SRLPDC 2024–25: 2 Deafblind manual interpreters, 1 guide communicator. Tiny bench.

Any-number VRS already exists inside the UK. The design stops at the border. Existence is not take-up.

1 Duty

BSL (Wales) Bill passed 11 Mar 2026. Equality Act remains the individual hook.

2 Payer

Local health boards. No Wales-wide isolated BSL spend found.

3 Booker

Board books F2F/VRI. Cardiff & Vale added Convo VRS to switchboard — organisation-level, not national.

4 VRS

999 BSL. NHS 111 Wales BSL via SignVideo 08:00–midnight on the official contact page. Not 24/7, not any-number.

5 Deafblind

NRCPD Wales = 1 RIDB. No fulfilment series.

Correct the earlier “no 111 BSL in Wales” lead. Limited hours is still not a national VRS.

1 Duty

ADA Title IV: FCC must ensure functionally equivalent calls. Hospitals have a separate ADA on-site/VRI duty.

2 Payer

TRS Fund net $1.480bn FY2025–26 (carrier levy). User pays $0 for the relay leg. Broadband is the hidden cost.

3 Booker

User dials any number on VRS. Clinic separately books the person in the room.

4 VRS

Any-number, 24/7, answer-time rules. The design Ofcom declined in 2011.

5 Deafblind

TRS includes Deafblind users. iCanConnect distributes equipment — no UK equivalent. Tactile clinical work still clinic duty.

Steal the phone-layer design. Do not paste the dollar figure into an NHS case. Two pots remain two pots.

1 Duty

Public bodies must use a qualified interpreter. Patient can request; clinician assesses. Family is not the model.

2 Payer

Health service pays. Free to the patient in public care (dental exception). NAV also funds work/education/daily-life hours.

3 Booker

The health service books. Screen/phone allowed, not a legal substitute by default.

4 VRS

Video interpreting is an extension of the public interpreter service — not a US telecoms product.

5 Deafblind

Inside the public service in principle. Headcount/fulfilment not opened as a series here.

Closest legal contrast: the clinic is already on the hook. Fulfilment data still missing — do not romanticise.

1 Duty

Regional responsibility under health law (HSL).

2 Payer

The region pays via its tolkcentral. Patient does not pay the interpreter in public care.

3 Booker

One specialist desk per region — ICS-comparable geography. Not a spoken-language multi-lot prime as the default.

4 VRS

National video interpreting exists; qualitative research describes queues. Not “no wait.”

5 Deafblind

Design runs through the same centre. Tactile wait series not opened here.

Ask whether any English ICB runs a dedicated BSL/Deafblind centre rather than DAL → Sign Solutions → DBE.

1 Duty

Right to gebarentolk / schrijftolk / tactile NGT for work, school and private life, including a doctor. Care providers also arrange in their own services.

2 Payer

UWV awards hours (OCW/SZW/VWS). Tolkcontact pays invoices.

3 Booker

User books a named interpreter from awarded hours. Provider may book clinical sessions they run.

4 VRS

KPN Teletolk. Hours, not a US any-number TRS, are the scarce resource.

5 Deafblind

Tactile NGT is a named category. Some schemes give Deafblind a much higher private-hour cap (168 vs 30 in a parallel scheme).

Shows hours-in-the-pocket clearly. England’s AtW is the cousin — work-only, capped, frozen, and not a GP booking.

1 Duty

Right to an Auslan interpreter. Health Department primary-care review found use suboptimal — awareness, procedure, workforce, split funding.

2 Payer

NDIS if eligible; NSLP via Deaf Connect for 65+ (50+ First Nations) not on NDIS; NABS for some private medical; EAF at work. TIS National = spoken languages only.

3 Booker

Whoever’s pot applies that day. Services Australia added on-demand Auslan video in its own centres (Dec 2025).

4 VRS

National Relay Service includes video relay. Separate from the person in the GP room.

5 Deafblind

Named in NABS/NSLP text. No national tactile fulfilment series opened here.

What to steal for SWL — and what not to

  1. Scotland’s any-number VRS as proof the design is possible inside the UK. Do not ignore the 60% never-used figure.
  2. Norway’s named clinic-booker statute. Do not invent Nordic wait times.
  3. Sweden’s one desk per region. FOI whether any English ICB has one.
  4. Netherlands naming tactile as its own category. Do not import annual hour-caps into NHS appointments.
  5. US levy + functional equivalence for the phone layer only. Do not treat $1.48bn as an NHS line.
  6. Australia’s GP booking flowchart and the “rights ≠ use” finding. Do not copy the pot-split.

Correction logged this build

An earlier lead treated Wales NHS 111 BSL as possibly absent. Official NHS 111 Wales “Contact us” states SignVideo access seven days, 08:00–midnight, with Relay UK as the non-BSL path. Welsh Ambulance confirms 999 BSL. Status: limited-hours health VRS, not “no service,” not any-number.
