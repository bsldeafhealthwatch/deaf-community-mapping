RNID
Manage Closely
The UK's largest Deaf charity, supporting 18 million people. Co-authored the Still Ignored NHS access report with SignHealth. High national policy influence — a key partner for any accessibility innovation seeking NHS pathway adoption or institutional legitimacy. Led the BSL Act 2022 campaign with the BDA.
Co-develop pilots NHS access campaigns Policy endorsement BSL standards input
SignHealth
Manage Closely
The dedicated Deaf health charity — the most directly aligned nationally for healthcare accessibility work. Jointly led the Still Ignored report; commissioned by National Deaf CAMHS. Their NHS-connected work makes them the primary route into healthcare commissioning. Also worked with Healthwatch SWL on the Accessible Information Standard review across the SW London ICB boroughs.
Primary partnership target NHS commissioning routes User research access AIS compliance
SWLSTG National Deaf Services
Manage Closely
South West London and St George's Mental Health NHS Trust delivers the National Deaf Mental Health Service from Springfield University Hospital in Tooting — directly inside the SWL ICB footprint. This is the NHS body providing specialist Deaf adult and CAMHS inpatient and community mental health services nationally, with BSL-fluent staff on site. The Deaf Adult Community Team operates Monday–Friday from Newton Building, Springfield. They accept GP, voluntary sector, and self-referrals — making this a critical local NHS gateway partner.
NHS gateway into deaf mental health Self-referral accepted BSL-fluent clinical team on site Tooting — your closest NHS deaf service

British Society for Mental Health & Deaf People
Keep Satisfied
The only UK charity solely focused on mental health for Deaf people. Clinical credibility and NHS relationships are strong. Has existing ties to SWLSTG National Deaf Services — the Springfield Deaf team has presented at BSMHD conferences. A valuable endorser and network connector rather than direct delivery partner.
Clinical endorsement SWLSTG connection Conference & network access
Healthwatch South West London
Keep Satisfied
Healthwatch organisations across Sutton, Merton, Wandsworth, Richmond and Kingston conducted the Accessible Information Standard review for the SWL ICB, directly leading to the new DA Languages BSL interpreter service rolling out to all GP practices in October 2024. They hold the patient voice bridge between the Deaf community and the ICB. Healthwatch Sutton specifically references SignHealth and Royal Association for Deaf People as accessibility resources for GP practices. A high-leverage partner for influencing ICB commissioning decisions.
ICB commissioning influence AIS compliance monitoring GP practice accessibility Patient voice amplifier

Pilot partner — West Sussex User testing access NHS audiology referrals

The most directly relevant local grassroots body for community user testing of doorbell alert systems and home accessibility tools in the Sutton / Merton area.
Closest local DDPO to Sutton Home independence tools Community user testing Health advocacy referrals

Colliers Wood Deaf Club
Monitor
A grassroots social club for Deaf people in Colliers Wood, Merton — one of your closest local community touchpoints. Based at The Guardian Centre on Clarendon Road, SW19. Low formal power and not a healthcare delivery body, but an authentic community anchor and word-of-mouth channel. Oak Lodge School
Monitor
A maintained special school for D/deaf students aged 10–19 in Wandsworth, rated Good by Ofsted (2025). Also hosts Deaf First advice/advocacy and a Lifelong Learning programme for deaf adults. Draws students from 25 boroughs. Not a health delivery body, but a significant local hub for the Deaf community with adult learner access. Useful for longer-term awareness and community building.
Deaf adult lifelong learning Community hub — Wandsworth Schools awareness pathway
