RNID
Manage Closely
The UK's largest Deaf charity, supporting 18 million people. Co-authored the Still Ignored NHS report with SignHealth. High national policy influence — a key partner for any accessibility innovation seeking institutional legitimacy or NHS pathway adoption. Their BSL Act 2022 campaign shows legislative reach.
Co-develop pilots NHS access campaigns Policy endorsement BSL standards input
SignHealth
Manage Closely
The dedicated Deaf health charity. Uniquely focused on where healthcare meets the Deaf community — mental health, domestic abuse, NHS access. The most directly aligned organisation for any healthcare accessibility tool or doorbell alert project. Their NHS-commissioned work gives direct healthcare system connection.
Primary partnership target Clinical testing NHS commissioning routes User research access
National Deaf Children's Society
Keep Satisfied
High institutional power and reach but primary focus is children and young people rather than adult healthcare access. Relevant where solutions serve families — particularly EHCP processes, home safety alerts, and school environments. A useful route into local authority and SEND systems.
Family-facing solutions EHCP & SEND pathways School environment alerts
British Society for Mental Health & Deaf People
Keep Satisfied
The only UK charity solely focused on mental health for Deaf people. Clinical credibility and NHS relationships are strong, but operational scope is narrow and specialist. Interest in accessibility tools is conditional on mental health relevance. A valuable endorser rather than delivery partner.
Clinical endorsement Mental health tool validation Conference presence
Action for Deafness
Keep Informed
Delivers NHS audiology services directly in Sussex — geographically your closest relevant partner from Sutton and West Sussex. Rated 4.8★ with strong community trust. High operational interest in accessibility solutions but limited national influence. Ideal early-adopter pilot partner for doorbell alert systems.
Pilot partner — West Sussex User testing access NHS audiology referrals Local community reach
