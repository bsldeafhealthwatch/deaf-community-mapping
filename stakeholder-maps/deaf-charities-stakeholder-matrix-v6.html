v6 restoration note (Sept 2026): v5's rebuild onto a JS data-array model dropped 16 organisations that existed in v4 without any record of an intentional removal — the file simply carried forward a subset of entries. All 16 are restored in v6: BID Services, Action for Deafness, DeafBlind UK, Croydon Hearing Resource Centre, Deaf First (Oak Lodge), RAD Croydon Caseworker Service, Oak Lodge School, Colliers Wood Deaf Club, Croydon Deaf Club, six borough Healthwatch entries (Croydon, Lambeth, Bromley, Greenwich, Lewisham, Southwark, Bexley), RAD Lambeth Service, DASL, Buddies All CIC, and the Bromley Chain dissolved-organisation context entry. v6 also adds five new entries reflecting Deaf-Blind and BSL-specific access needs: Sense (the sector's only NHS VCSE Health and Wellbeing Alliance member), NRCPD (the national BSL interpreter register — 1,632 registered interpreters, a hard capacity constraint), a flagged deafblind evidence cluster (Sense's ~450,000 estimate plus an unresolved 19-vs-8 interpreter count discrepancy), and BSL/Deafblind badges applied across existing entries where language-access or dual-sensory relevance applies. New filter buttons (BSL, Deafblind, ⚠ Unverified) surface these across all tabs.

Interpreting Agencies tracker (added Sept 2026): A new "🎧 Interpreting Agencies" tab tracks the actual BSL and Deafblind interpreting service providers operating in or near South London, separate from the charity/stakeholder power-interest matrix, because the useful question for these is service status (live contract, gap, or unconfirmed) rather than power/interest. Four agencies researched and added: DA Languages (confirmed live on two separate NHS contracts — all SWL GP practices since Oct 2024, and the SEL Primary Care Interpreting Service for Lambeth/Lewisham/Southwark since Mar 2020 — with a confirmed, named gap covering Bexley/Bromley/Greenwich residents); Remark! (the Deaf-led national booking agency, distinct from Remark! Community's grassroots clubs already tracked separately — offers both BSL and deafblind interpreter booking); Deafblind Enablement (a specialist agency solely focused on deafblind interpreting, holding NHS Trust contracts nationally, though none confirmed in South London yet); and Deaf Umbrella (a London/South East BSL agency with no confirmed NHS or deafblind work — flagged as unconfirmed for this project's health-access purpose, not excluded). Sourced from each agency's own published service pages and Healthwatch Sutton/Kingston/Richmond/SEL ICS official announcements, Sept 2026.

APPA correction & addition (Sept 2026, Grok deep research + Companies House cross-check): "APPA UK Ltd" was flagged as a missing agency but does not exist as a current UK interpreting provider — Companies House confirms that name belongs to an unrelated, dissolved Scottish medical-goods retailer (SC231577, wound up 2007). The company actually operating a BSL service under a similar name is Appa Me Ltd (APPA ME LIMITED, company no. 07202345, active, Ilford-registered, teaching from Lewisham in the SEL ICB area). Added to the tracker with a "partial" status: it is primarily a Signature-accredited BSL/interpreter training provider rather than a confirmed NHS interpreting supplier — no NHS, ICB or council contract was found in Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, or RNID's own published agency list — and Deafblind interpreting, though initially suspected, is not confirmed as a delivered service (only signposted via an external resources page). A 2016 NRCPD minute flagging a training-certification concern is recorded as unresolved historical context, not a current issue. Full source list: Companies House (07202345 and SC231577), appa.me.uk, Trustpilot, LinkedIn, CQC, Find a Tender/Contracts Finder, RNID's agency list, and NRCPD's September 2016 Practitioners' Forum minutes.

Full agency sweep & confirmed Bexley/Bromley/Greenwich gap (Sept 2026, Grok deep research pass): A systematic search across NHS trust pages, ICB/council pages, Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, and framework award notices confirmed the three-borough SEL gap already flagged on the DA Languages entry is a documented publication gap, not an unknown — no ICB-wide, borough-wide, or named GP-contract BSL/Deafblind provider exists in the public record for Bexley, Bromley or Greenwich. What those residents do have instead: NHS 111 BSL (24/7 advice, not a booked GP interpreter) and SignHealth's SEL-wide BSL Talking Therapies contract (therapy, not general interpreting — see the updated SignHealth entry above). Nine new agencies added to the Interpreting Agencies tab as a result: Sign Solutions (the actual named BSL video subcontractor behind both DA Languages GP contracts, via InterpretersLive!); LanguageLine Solutions (the former SWL GP prime, now holding Croydon Health Services/Epsom & St Helier/Kingston & Richmond hospital contracts, though BSL isn't itemised separately in those notices); thebigword (Kingston & Richmond children's services only, not health); three civic-desk/VRS entries — Royal Greenwich Interpreting Service, Kingston Interpreting Service, and Convo/SignLive — plus Significan't UK Ltd t/a SignVideo (now Sorenson), the vendor behind NHS 111 BSL; and three framework-eligible-but-no-local-award entries — Signalise Co-op (Deaf-owned, genuinely actionable via a direct NHS SBS Lot 2 framework call-off to help close the SEL gap), Clarion Interpreting (MoJ tactile-communications framework holder; its own GSTT/King's healthcare claim is a dated, unverified self-description), and Sign Language Interactions. Two further entries record known-unknowns rather than active services: BSL Healthcare (a patient-facing booking site with no confirmed company identity or NHS award behind it) and a bundled entry for Positive Signs, Involve Interpreter and BSL First (all three appear on RNID's national agency list with no confirmed South London presence). Also noted but not added as a service entry: King's £1.2m interpreting tender (explicitly including BSL, covering Denmark Hill, PRUH and Queen Mary's Sidcup) was cancelled 6 August 2026 — a significant unresolved gap at a major SEL acute trust, worth monitoring for re-tender. Guy's & St Thomas', Lewisham & Greenwich, Croydon Health Services, Epsom & St Helier and Oxleas trust pages all advertise BSL access without naming the supplying agency — these are the highest-yield FOI targets, together with the three Bexley/Bromley/Greenwich Place primary-care teams directly.

Deafblind interpreting capacity resolved — the 19-vs-8 discrepancy (Sept 2026, Grok deep research pass): Both figures were real, but were snapshots from different dates on the same NRCPD register, not competing claims — see the substantially rewritten "Deafblind interpreting capacity — evidence & register data" entry in Broader Ecosystem for the full timeline (12 in ~2022, then 8 from Feb 2025 through the 23 June 2026 GOV.UK funding announcement, then 19 on the live NRCPD table as of 5 Sept 2026, as competency-assessment weekends add names). The load-bearing number for South London planning: only 4 of the current 19 RIDBs nationally sit in London + South East combined, and only 1 in London itself — that, not the reassuring-looking national 19, is the correct local capacity baseline. The NRCPD and Sense entries above were also updated: NRCPD's general RSLI count refreshed to 1,657 (from 1,632) with the RIDB breakdown added; Sense's entry now distinguishes its ~451,211 dual-sensory-loss prevalence estimate from the government's separate, much narrower ~12,000 "communication needs" figure, and documents its communicator-guide/intervenor service model as distinct from (not a substitute for) RIDB clinical interpreting. Critical operational finding folded into the DA Languages, Sign Solutions and Deafblind Enablement entries: DA Languages and LanguageLine do not hold their own deafblind interpreting bench — both subcontract deafblind work to Deafblind Enablement, meaning DBE's own national capacity (part of that same 4-in-London+SE figure) is the actual ceiling on any deafblind booking under the South London GP contracts, regardless of which prime the booking nominally runs through. Deafblind Enablement's own new Professional Register for Deafblind Specialists (launched 29 Jun 2026) and its likely match to the Moorfields Eye Hospital good-practice example cited in the BSL Advisory Board's Locked Out report (27 Nov 2025) are both recorded on its entry. Two further additions: Sight for Surrey, a communicator-guide provider directly adjacent to Kingston/Richmond/Sutton — genuinely well-placed for community support, but explicitly not a substitute for clinical RIDB interpreting — added to the Interpreting Agencies tab; and Action for Deafness's pre-merger Hertfordshire communicator-guide contract (£904,000, awarded after the previous provider gave sudden notice) added to its existing entry as a concrete illustration of how thin the CG market is nationally, not just in London. RAD's and BID's entries were also updated with newly-confirmed deafblind interpreter booking services (RAD) and 19,643 NHS-inclusive interpreting hours for 2023-24 (BID), both flagged as unconfirmed for South London specifically. Full source list for this pass: NRCPD's live registration-figures table (5 Sep 2026), NRCPD's fee schedule, GOV.UK's 23 June 2026 funding announcement, the BSL Advisory Board/DHSC Locked Out report and "18 areas for action" (27 Nov 2025), Liam O'Dell's reporting (12 Feb 2025) on the Sarah Reed petition, Community Care Inform (24 Jan 2024), Sense's own published prevalence figures, and each named agency's own site (Deafblind Enablement, Remark!, RAD, BID Services, Sorenson UK, Sight for Surrey).
