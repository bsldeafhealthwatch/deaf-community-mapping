deafPLUS
Keep Informed — upgrade to Manage Closely for doorbell project
Established 1971 as Breakthrough (Deaf-Hearing Integration) — one of the UK's oldest Deaf support organisations. Now operating as deafPLUS with offices in Bromley, Ealing, Whitechapel, Birmingham, Bath, Hampshire and Surrey. Confirmed active February 2026.

Bromley office (confirmed Jan 2025): The most strategically important office for this project. After Bromley Chain's formal dissolution (March 2024), its assets transferred to deafPLUS — making deafPLUS the direct successor of all Bromley Chain community activity. The Bromley office runs: a Hard of Hearing social group (2nd and 4th Monday, with cake and guest speakers); a BSL practice group (all levels, hearing welcome); a tinnitus support group; and crucially — a Hearing Equipment Demonstration Service where people can try and assess assistive devices. Text/SMS: 07732 449349. Office open Mon–Fri 9:30am–4pm by appointment.

National reach: Offices in Bromley, Ealing and Whitechapel across London, plus Birmingham, Bath, Hampshire and Surrey. The Surrey office covers the Sutton–West Sussex corridor. Supported by deaf ambassador Rose Ayling-Ellis (EastEnders) and Gok Wan. Services include IAG, advocacy, employment and training, lipreading rehabilitation, DeafBlind support, and equipment assessment.

For healthcare work: deafPLUS has long-standing NHS partnerships for equipment assessment and IAG. For the doorbell alert project: their equipment demonstration service model in Bromley is the closest existing analogue in South London to what the project intends to do at scale.

deafPLUS — Bromley ★
New Addition — Keep Informed · SEL Bromley
Confirmed active as of January 2025. The direct successor to Bromley Chain (dissolved March 2024) — deafPLUS received the Bromley Chain's assets and now provides the strongest formal Deaf support presence in the Bromley borough. Formerly known as Breakthrough (Deaf-Hearing Integration), established 1971.

Bromley services confirmed: Hard of Hearing social group (2nd and 4th Monday, with cake and guest speakers); BSL practice group (all levels, hearing welcome); tinnitus support group; and a Hearing Equipment Demonstration Service — where people try and compare assistive devices in person. Office open Monday–Friday 9:30am–4pm by appointment. Contact: 07732 449349 (text/SMS only).

The equipment demonstration service model is the closest thing currently operating in South London to what the doorbell alert project aims to do. The Bromley social groups also provide direct access to the demographic most likely to benefit from home alert systems.

Bromley Chain — DISSOLVED
Dissolved March 2024 → See deafPLUS Bromley
Bromley Chain (charity number 281051) was formally dissolved by the Charity Commission on 19 March 2024. It ran the Bromley Deaf Social Club, Bromley Deaf Children's Society, Deaf Access advisory service, and administered the Phyllis Kither Bequest small grants fund. On dissolution, its assets transferred to Breakthrough (Deaf-Hearing Integration) — charity 1073468 — which operates as deafPLUS.

This is not a loss — deafPLUS Bromley has expanded what Bromley Chain offered, adding a professional office with weekday IAG support and a formal equipment demonstration service. The Phyllis Kither Bequest grants fund may also continue under deafPLUS — worth asking when making contact, as it could potentially fund a doorbell alert pilot in Bromley.
