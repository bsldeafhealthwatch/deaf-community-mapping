## Verdict for any public page

**Curate first. Draft almost nothing.**

The one sentence a page is allowed to lead with, if any:

> Outsourcing the helpdesk does not switch off the employer’s Equality Act duty. Access to Work can fund a Deaf employee’s own video-relay minutes. ## Don't list (print near any workplace / AtW / VRS page)

- Do not print that a named employer, outsourcing provider or procurement contract is unlawful, discriminatory or in breach of the Equality Act without a named statute, regulation or judgment **on these facts**. There isn’t one in this batch.
- Do not print “the duty is discharged because we outsourced IT.” s.20 / s.39 do not say that (prompt 121).
- Do not print “the contractor has no Equality Act duty.” s.31(5) can put Part 3 on a closed staff service. Fact-specific.
- Do not print Sch. 3 para 20 as wiping **Part 5** off a pension helpline. It disapplies **s.29** (Part 3) for employer-arranged insurance and personal-pension services only.
- Do not print para 20 as covering every occupational-scheme administrator. The statute says personal pension scheme (Pension Schemes Act 1993 s.1). Occupational administration is not named.
- Do not print PCR 2015 reg. 42(8) as the rule for every live 2026 public contract. New covered procurements from 24 Feb 2025 run under the Procurement Act.
- Do not print Procurement Act s.56 as “there is no accessibility requirement anymore.” The Act dropped the 42(8) sentence; PSED and the Equality Act still apply. Call that a relocation, not a repeal.
- Do not print the 2026 Services Code as “not yet law.” It commenced 5 August 2026 (SI 2026/788). It does not create a new duty. It restates the s.31(5) split.
- Do not print “most UK staff helpdesks are voice-only” as a measured rate. Prompt 122: no census.
- Do not print Sign Solutions / InterpretersLive! hours or intranet-button copy as an official process or as evidence of coverage.
- Do not file customer-facing VRS (Barnet, Three, Virgin Media, Fidelity on the Sign Solutions clients page) as staff-desk evidence.
- Do not print a WCAG-conformant intranet as proof the *voice* desk is reachable. Lockout-blocks-the-ticket remains an operational risk statement, not a documented incident.
- Do not print Safecall’s written-portal sentence as “the UK standard workaround” for VRS-versus-anonymity. One vendor; no published reconciliation of a named BSL interpreter seeing the report.
- Do not print “Protect offers BSL VRS.” Protect’s own site is phone + form. The BSL sentence is a LawWorks directory entry (17 Apr 2026) about arranging translation if asked. Charity advice line, not a commercial ethics hotline.
- Do not print GOV.UK whistleblowing-for-employers guidance as if it named BSL. It does not (6 Apr 2026).
- Do not print AtW as a fund that varies a managed-service contract or stands up a VRS seat on the contractor desk (prompt 124).
- Do not print AtW VRS bundles as available to civil servants. Staff-guide introduction edited 13 November 2025: civil servants can no longer apply.
- Do not reprint the £69,260 cap, 3,210 BSL-interpreter customers, £18,200 average, or NAO 109-day wait as discoveries of this batch. Those live in 20 / 52–54 / 116.
- Do not print RNID 2026 / AWS0095 / AWS0121 / Sense ATW0075 (2014) as helpdesk-budget evidence. Wrong cell.
- Do not print Section 508 603.3, Canadian federal justification-if-omitted rules, Directive 2014/24 Art. 42(1), the EAA, or Australian EAF as UK duties (prompt 125).
- Do not print Barclays’ supplier WCAG guide as a helpdesk-VRS contract term.
- Do not reuse Rimmer, Clarke, Iqbal, Bentley, Bussey, Clinton, Graham, Fletcher, or NDCS childhood cases as outsourced-desk testimony (prompt 126).
- Do not generalise vendor marketing or BDF *customer*-channel advice into staff-desk first-person.
- Do not collapse this batch into 78–80, 97–102, or 141–149.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment — whose contract, whose wallet, whose script.

## What to do with this finding

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| Someone asks “is it legal that the offshore desk is phone-only?” | “The employer’s duty is not switched off by the contract. Whether any particular step is reasonable is fact-specific. | Someone asks “who pays for the interpreter on that call?” | Three wallets: AtW minutes for *that employee* (if eligible, if awarded, if not a civil servant); employer contract variation; contractor line item — usually unpriced. Do not say AtW will pay the supplier. |
| Someone is locked out and cannot raise a ticket | Practical doors, not law: written fallback that works *logged-out*; Relay UK; employee-side VRS if the award includes it; line manager / onshore resolver. Do not draft a national protocol. |

## Cross-batch rules

- **Distinct from 78–80** because those are general VRS / telecoms access (home, office, travel, airlines). This batch is whether the *employer’s contracted internal desk* was specified to accept a non-voice caller.
- **Distinct from 97–102** because those are the disability-adjustment *ticket*. This batch is the ordinary channel you ring when you are locked out, not the RA form.
- **Distinct from 109–114** because grievance is what happens after a refusal. This batch stops at “the desk cannot take the call.”
- **Distinct from 115–120** because DeafBlind in-post content is the adjustment product. A DeafBlind employee hitting the same phone-only desk is this batch; RIDB / CG tariffs stay in 115–116.
- **Distinct from 141–149** because EAP is a confidential outsourced *support* product. Do not preview EAP findings here.
- **Shared facts that must not be reprinted as discoveries of 121–126:** AtW cap £69,260; BSL bands £26–47 / London £30–52; VRS bundles; 3,210 BSL-interpreter customers; civil-servant AtW exclusion (also relevant to 97 / 116); InterpretersLive! hours already used on SEL / SWL GP contracts; Rimmer / Iqbal / Bentley.
- **Held-back:** none inside 121–126. Next issued range already on the index is 127–133 (Chinese Walls). Do not spend 150 on a seventh outsourced-channel prompt. If a Model Services Contract clause or a named lockout quotation appears, patch these files.
