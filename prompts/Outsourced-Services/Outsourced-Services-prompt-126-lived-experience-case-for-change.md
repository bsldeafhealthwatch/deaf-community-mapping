# Prompt 126 — Lived experience of outsourced internal channels, and any policy ask

**Master index prompt 126.** Lived-experience file in the Outsourced / offshored internal service-channel batch (prompts 121–126). Compiled 11 September 2026. Dossier rule: stay unbiased; follow money and fulfilment; do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. An unsourced AI claim is the lowest tier. Gaps are logged as gaps, not filled guesses.

**What this prompt is for.** Find first-person UK testimony from Deaf BSL-using or DeafBlind *employees* about an outsourced or offshored internal channel (IT helpdesk, payroll, facilities, whistleblowing, pensions/benefits) where they were told no accessible route existed or that “third party” cost was the barrier. Record any named campaign or policy ask aimed at this layer. Do not reuse JCP, tribunal-hearing, AtW-backlog or end-of-life testimony from other batches.

**Index check.** Prompts 101, 107, 113 and 119 already logged how thin in-post first-person is in neighbouring cells. This file is a different object (ordinary internal channel, not the adjustment pipeline).

## Headline

**UK in-post first-person on this object is unpublished on this pass.** No named Deaf BSL-using or DeafBlind employee account was found that describes ringing an outsourced IT desk, payroll shared-service, facilities line, ethics hotline or pension administrator and being told there was no BSL / VRS route or that a third-party interpreter was “not in the contract.” Adjacent published testimony stays in its own cell: Rimmer is Jobcentre Plus (prompt 33); Iqbal is Home Office office-hours BSL and occupational health (prompt 92); RNID/DeafATW 2026 and AWS0095 are Access to Work administration; Bentley is tribunal-hearing administration (prompt 110/114); Locked Out is health and public services.

**Named campaign, consultation response, trade-union submission or policy ask specifically about accessible *internal service channels* for Deaf or DeafBlind employees: not found.** BDF’s Accessible Technology Charter asks members to require accessible products from technology supply partners — procurement of *systems*, not a helpdesk VRS SKU. Sign Solutions sells an intranet VRS button as a commercial offer (prompt 122), which is not a campaign. Locked Out (27 November 2025) and Still Ignored ask for BSL in health and public-facing services; they do not run a chapter on outsourced staff desks.

The case for change that *can* be written from this batch without inventing a witness is the structural one already logged in 121 and 124: the employer duty survives outsourcing; AtW was not built to retrofit the contractor; no published UK protocol closes the loop. That is a gap statement. It is not a lived-experience quotation.

**Hard-don't.** Do not reuse Bussey, Clinton, Graham, Rimmer, Clarke, Iqbal, Bentley, Fletcher or the NDCS childhood cases. Do not generalise vendor marketing or BDF customer-channel advice into staff-desk testimony. Do not fill the UK cell with US ADA helpdesk stories.

## 126.1 Thin is the result

Four neighbouring lived-experience files (101, 107, 113, 119) already showed that UK Deaf workplace first-person clusters around Access to Work, Jobcentre Plus and a handful of tribunal-hearing admin failures. This pass did not find a fifth cluster on the outsourced internal channel. That is consistent with prompt 122’s observation that staff-desk accessibility is rarely a published research object.

Do not manufacture a composite “Deaf employees told us the offshore desk could not help.” A composite is an unsourced AI claim.

## 126.2 What a later pass would need

A usable case for change on this layer would be one named employee, one named channel type, one stated reason (“not on the contract” / “no budget for a third party” / “we only do phone”), and a date. Until that exists, the public-facing product from this batch is the legal-and-money map in 121 and 124, not a testimony page.
