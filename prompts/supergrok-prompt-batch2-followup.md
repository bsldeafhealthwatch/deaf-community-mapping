## HOW TO USE THIS PROMPT

Paste the entire block below into SuperGrok. This is a **follow-up batch**, not a repeat of the original research prompt — a Claude-based research pass has already directly verified most of the original prompt's five sections against primary sources (NHS England, GOV.UK, RNID, Sense, Healthwatch, legislation.gov.uk). This batch is deliberately aimed at:

1. **The specific gaps that direct primary-source retrieval could not close** (listed below with exactly what's missing and why)
2. **Real-time / social signal that only live search can catch** — recent posts, announcements, and grassroots chatter from charities, campaigners, and affected people, which a one-off document fetch will miss
3. **Breadth across many small local sources at once** (individual ICBs, councils, local Healthwatch branches) where casting a wide net matters more than opening any single document in full

For every item, give: source name, URL, and exact date. Flag anything older than 12 months. Where you cite a claim from X/social media, name the account and give the post date/link — do not paraphrase a post as if it were a verified fact; flag it as "social claim, unverified" if you cannot corroborate it against a document.

You are a specialist NHS policy and Deaf community health research assistant with live web and social (X) search access. I am building a stakeholder matrix and policy briefing for the **Deaf Network Health Group**, a project within the **South West London Integrated Care Board (SWL ICB)** footprint, covering the boroughs of **Croydon, Kingston upon Thames, Merton, Richmond upon Thames, Sutton, and Wandsworth**.

A prior research pass already directly confirmed the following — do NOT re-research these, treat them as settled background:
- Cabinet Office PPN 001 (12 Feb 2025) sets a 1 April 2026 VCSE spend target for central government departments and NDPBs, but **explicitly excludes NHS trusts and foundation trusts**.
- NHS Provider Selection Regime has no VCSE-specific direct-award route or threshold; VCSE providers use the same five processes as any other provider.
- Section 256, NHS Act 2006 remains in force but does not explicitly name voluntary organisations as a payment category.
- RNID's Jan 2026 FOI: 92% of NHS staff in England have not completed Accessible Information Standard training.
- Healthwatch South West London's own report confirms 878 BSL interpretation requests from SWL GP practices, Oct 2024–Jun 2025 (Wandsworth 466, Croydon 185), and that DA Languages/Sign Solutions took over the SWL GP BSL interpreting contract from 1 October 2024, funded via the South West London Innovation ICP Priorities Fund.
- BSL user population figures are contested: RNID's own current page gives 22,000 (2021 Census, main language) / ~25,000 (RNID UK-wide estimate) / 151,000 (BDA's wider figure); the previously-used 73,000 figure does not appear in any current RNID source.
- Sense's 450,000 UK deafblind estimate is a 2017-vintage Operational Research Society model, not a fresh count; Deafblind UK's Aug 2025 impact report is fully logged (4,346 members supported, 9,312 helpline calls).

### SECTION D3 — Breadth sweep across smaller/local sources

10. **Other London ICBs' Deaf/BSL provision, for comparison.** Quickly sweep (do not go deep) North West London, South East London, North Central London, North East London, and East London ICBs for any public BSL/Deaf-specific interpreting service announcement, similar to SWL's DA Languages/Sign Solutions contract.
