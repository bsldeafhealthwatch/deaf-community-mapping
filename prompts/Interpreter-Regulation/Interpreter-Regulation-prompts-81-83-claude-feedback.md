## Verdict for any public page

**Curate first. Draft almost nothing.**

The one sentence the page is allowed to lead with:

> NRCPD and RBSLI are voluntary registers. NHS and other public contracts choose to require them (AIS 1.32 is the live NHS hook). There is no UK statute that makes unregistered BSL interpreting a criminal offence. On 10 September 2026 the NRCPD public table still showed 19 Registered Interpreters for Deafblind People nationally, with none listed in the East Midlands, South West, or Yorkshire and The Humber.

Thin original drafts worth doing *later*, after a human signs this file off:

1. A **one-screen “voluntary register, contract requirement”** box. That distinction *is* the finding (prompt 81).
2. A **four-line “confirm the interpreter’s NRCPD/RBSLI number before we proceed”** line for a GP/hospital letter. Do not write “illegal if unregistered.”

## Don’t list (print near any interpreter / NRCPD page)

- Do not write “using an unregistered interpreter is illegal.”
- Do not write “NRCPD is the statutory regulator.” It is a charity/company holding voluntary registers. Statutory regulation is NRCPD’s long-term aim with no published timetable.
- Do not write “there are now 68 RIDB.” GOV.UK 23 June 2026 grant *targets* 68. Live table 10 September 2026: **19**. Historic baseline in the press copy was **8**.
- Do not add NRCPD category totals to produce a unique-person headcount. The table says it includes duals. Use the homepage live feed (**2,087** on this pass) when a single total is required, and date it.
- Do not reprint RBSLI “52” from a 2019 paper as current. No 2025–26 headcount found.
- Do not write “Ofqual licences BSL interpreters.” Ofqual regulates Signature as an awarding organisation and INTRA6 (RQF 603/5484/7) as a qualification. Registration is a second gate.
- Do not collapse the BSL GCSE (school language product, Ofqual rules 13 November 2025) with RSLI.
- Do not write “the AIS duty transfers to the agency.” SC12.3 binds the Provider. EA 2010 binds the service the patient is trying to use. PHSO held UHB and the GP practice, not the agency.
- Do not print RM6302 Lot 4 unit rates. Schedule 3 is not public. Do not invent a CCS BSL hourly rate.
- Do not recycle DAL ~37% *company-wide* gross margin as the haircut on a BSL booking.
- Do not print *Locked Out*’s “92% fulfilment KPI” as an official CCS figure until the framework schedule is opened. Board wording, contested.
- Do not print an official interpreter-to-Deaf ratio. None exists. ONS 2021 main language E&W is **22,000**. *Locked Out*/BDA 87,000 / 151,000 are different denominators. Researcher arithmetic (~15 main-language users per E&W SLI cell) is not a dashboard tile.
- Do not print “20.6% accept healthcare / 7.65% mental health” as NRCPD administrative data. Those are *Locked Out* Board-cited percentages.
- Do not write that England has a funded general-RSLI workforce plan. The only named growth pound found is the **£15,000** RIDB assessment grant.
- Do not import NHS England *clinical staffing* agency price caps as a BSL tariff.
- Do not hold the page out as a regulator, a booking desk, or legal advice.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment. Headcount, hours, who pays, who was held by PHSO.

## What actually closes the gap

### 1. Use the registers as registers

Link NRCPD / RBSLI / SRLPDC search. Tell the reader to write down the ID number. That is the fulfilment check AIS implementation guidance already asks for when an agency is used.

### 2. Keep duty on the provider

Alan Graham (UHB, PHSO Feb 2026) and Samantha (GP, PHSO Aug 2026) are the public cases. Both hold the NHS body. Agency KPI recovery is a contract-management sentence, not a second duty-holder.

### 3. FOI — fold into the existing companion log

Do **not** build an interpreter-regulation letter wave. Add at most:

| Body | Ask |
|---|---|
| Named SWL/SEL trusts + ICB already on T1/T2 | Last FY: BSL bookings requested / fulfilled / cancelled by supplier; F2F vs VRI split; unit rate if held. Found / not-found / refused. |
| NRCPD (right of reply first) | Unique-person count by category and nation at a stated date; complaints received / closed / sanctions 2023–25. Reply overrides the public table for scoring. |
| CCS / one ICB | Is there a BSL-specific fulfilment KPI in RM6302 Lot 4 or the local call-off? What %? |

Do not FOI “how many unregistered interpreters worked in Croydon last year.” They will not have a clean field.

### 4. What not to build

No “AIS Compliance Monitor” product name (DAGM 42 already killed that). No RAG of trusts labelled “non-compliant with the Act” because they used an agency that missed a booking. No second FOI platform. No AI-filled NRCPD scrape presented as official statistics. Static HTML of the dated registration-figures table is enough.
