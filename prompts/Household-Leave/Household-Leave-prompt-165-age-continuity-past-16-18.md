# Prompt 165 — Age continuity: does the toolkit track developmental dependency past 16/18, or only chronological age?

## Headline

**The toolkit splits.** Unpaid parental leave is a chronological-age product: MAPLE 1999 reg. 15 — “An employee may not exercise any entitlement to parental leave in respect of a child after the date of the child’s 18th birthday.” That cutoff is the same whether or not the child is disabled. The disabled-child rule in the default scheme (Sch. 2 para. 7) only changes *how* leftover weeks may be taken (days rather than whole weeks). It does not add years.

**Carer’s leave and s.57A do not state an age ceiling on “dependant.”** ERA 1996 s.80J(2)(a) and s.57A(3)–(5) turn on relationship (spouse/civil partner, child, parent), same-household, or reasonable reliance. Neither section writes “under 18.” Equality Act disability and “care for more than three months” are the carer’s-leave long-term-need routes; old age is a third. A disabled 19-year-old who still lives at home, or who reasonably relies on the parent, is capable of being a dependant on the face of those texts. That is a statutory reading, not a judgment. No named ET/EAT this pass applies those sections to a developmentally delayed adult child.

**An EHCP in force after 18 does not extend parental leave, create carer’s leave, or prove s.57A.** Children and Families Act 2014 s.45–46: a plan *may* continue, in some cases to the end of the academic year of the 25th birthday; the LA *may* cease earlier on two grounds. “Runs to 25” is not automatic. The plan is an education-and-training instrument. Nothing in CFA 2014 or MAPLE says it is a key to the employment toolkit.

**Flexible working** has no dependant-age condition. Day-one request; any reason. The two-requests-per-12-months cap is per employee (150). Article 8 does not write an age band into private-sector employment leave (153).

**Still-alive correction to 150.** MAPLE Sch. 2 para. 7 now names DLA, armed forces independence payment **and personal independence payment**. PIP was inserted by SI 2013/388. 150 recorded the regulation as DLA-only. The 18th-birthday cutoff in reg. 15 is unaffected.

## 165.1 How the four rights behave at 16 and at 18

| Right | At 16 | At 18 | After 18 while EHCP still in force |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unpaid parental leave | Still available (until 18th birthday). Disabled-child day-units if DLA/PIP/AFIP. | **Ends** on the 18th birthday (reg. 15). | **Does not restart.** EHCP is irrelevant to this cutoff. |
| s.57A | Available if the person is a dependant on s.57A(3)–(5). School-incident limb (1)(e) still fits a pupil. | No statutory off-switch in s.57A(3). Same-household and “reasonably relies” limbs remain available on the text. (1)(e) becomes the unpublished question if the setting is a college. | Same. EHCP does not prove or disprove reliance. Planned appointments still outside s.57A (150/157). |
| Carer’s leave | Available if long-term care need (EA disability is enough). One week per employee per year, not per dependant (150). | No statutory off-switch in s.80J. A 19-year-old with an EA disability who lives at home or reasonably relies is inside the definition on the text. | Same. EHCP is not the long-term-care-need test — EA disability or 3-month need is. |
| Flexible working | Available. No dependant-age filter. | Available. | Available. Two-request cap unchanged. |
| Article 8 | Indirect only (153). | Same. | Same. |

16 is not an employment-leave cliff for any of the four rights. 18 is a cliff **only** for unpaid parental leave.

## 165.2 What a public write-up may and may not say

May say: parental leave stops at 18, disabled or not; carer’s leave and emergency dependants leave are written as relationship/reliance tests with no age in the section; an EHCP is not an employment-leave extender; no named judgment has applied those sections to this household.

May not say: the employer “must” still treat a 19-year-old as a dependant; the employer “may” stop treating them as one; “EHCPs run to 25 so the parent keeps parental leave”; Article 8 fills the gap after 18; 150’s no-stacking finding is reopened.
