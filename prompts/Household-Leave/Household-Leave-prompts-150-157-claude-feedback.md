## Verdict for any public page

**Curate first. Draft almost nothing.**

The one sentence a page is allowed to lead with, if any:

> The four statutory rights exist separately. None of them treats “employee + disabled partner + EHCP child” as one qualifying situation. Planned appointments sit outside emergency dependants leave. Access to Work pays for the employee’s own job, not for the household’s care.

What already exists and should be linked, not rewritten:

| Need | Point at |
|---|---|
| Flexible working request | GOV.UK + ACAS Code. Day-one GB from 6 Apr 2024. Two requests / 12 months per *employee*. Two-month decision clock. ERA 2025 reasonableness test **not live** (2027). |
| Time off for dependants | GOV.UK `/time-off-for-dependants` + ACAS. Per-incident, no annual cap. Planned hospital appointments are **out** on GOV.UK’s own example. *Qua v John Ford Morrison* [2003] ICR 482. |
| Unpaid parental leave | GOV.UK + MAPLE 1999. 18 weeks *per child*. Day-one GB from 6 Apr 2026. Default whole weeks unless the child is “entitled to a disability living allowance” (regulation text — not EHCP, not automatically PIP). |
| Carer’s leave | GOV.UK `/carers-leave` + ACAS. One week per employee per year. **Cannot** take a week per dependant. Day-one from 6 Apr 2024. No evidence required. Employer may postpone, not refuse. |
| Carer’s Allowance / earnings limit | Carers UK + GOV.UK. £86.45 / £204 net from 6 Apr 2026. Cliff edge. Not an employment-retention grant. |
| Care Act carer’s assessment | Care Act 2014 s.10(6)(a) — must have regard to whether the carer works. Parent-carers of disabled children sit on the Children Act track, not s.10. |
| Paid-leave campaign (adjacent) | Carers UK *A right to Carer’s Leave*; *Make Work Pay* consultation (closed 1 Sep 2026). That is pay and length, not a household unit of account. |
| Deaf employee’s own workplace access | Existing AtW / AIS / Domain 14 pages. Do not caption them as household-leave funding. |

Thin original drafts worth doing *later*, after a human signs this file off:

1. A **one-screen right-chooser** for this household: emergency → s.57A; planned disabled-child hospital slot with 3+ days’ notice → carer’s leave; longer block of childcare → parental leave (per child); pattern change → FW request. Four doors, four clocks, one week cap. No new law invented.
2. A **four-line BSL process card** only after a named employer’s pack is opened: how to give s.57A notice without a voice call; that a companion at the FW meeting is not an interpreter; who books the interpreter for the consultation. 151 found no ACAS model for this.
3. A **watch**, not a page, on (a) ERA 2025 s.9 commencement, (b) the unpaid-carers consultation response, (c) any rewrite of MAPLE Sch. 2 para. 7 from DLA to PIP/EHCP.

Do not write “the toolkit is not fit for purpose” as a legal conclusion. Do not write “employers must grant extra leave when two household members are disabled.”

## Don't list (print near any household / carers / flexible-working page)

- Do not print that an employer’s refusal, postponement, or process is unlawful, discriminatory, or an Article 8 breach without a named statute, regulation, or judgment.
- Do not print household-level stacking as a recognised category. 150’s finding is that it is not.
- Do not invent an unofficial aggregate cap (“you have used your caring leave”) and do not print that practice has built one. 152 found a menu, not an aggregate.
- Do not treat an EHCP as a statutory key for carer’s leave, s.57A, or the MAPLE disabled-child whole-week exception. Eligibility is in the named statute. MAPLE still says DLA.
- Do not treat Association discrimination (*Coleman*) or EA s.20 as the stacking answer.
- Do not print Article 8 as a free-standing ET claim against a private employer. *X v Y* [2004] EWCA Civ 662: s.6 gives no cause of action against a respondent that is not a public authority. *Turner* [2012] EWCA Civ 1470: the s.98 band is compatible with Article 8. Recoding *Dobson* as Article 8 is a citation error — it is EA s.19.
- Do not file *Hatton v Sutherland* or *Unison* [2017] UKSC 51 as Article 8 family-life authorities.
- Do not print that AtW covers household caring. The staff-guide “Carer” support worker is the *customer’s* mobility / toilet use at work, starting at the front door.
- Do not reprint the AtW cap, the 3,210 BSL-interpreter customers, or hourly bands in this batch. Those belong to 20–26 / 52–54 / 116.
- Do not print Carer’s Allowance as an employment-retention product. The £204 earnings limit is a constraint on paid work.
- Do not collapse a Children Act parent-carer assessment into Care Act s.10.
- Do not print “HSE requires a communicator-guide” or any HSE-must sentence from 117 into this cell.
- Do not paste FMLA 12 weeks, EU 2019/1158 five days, Irish 104 weeks, Norwegian extra omsorgsdager, Swedish närståendepenning, or Legge 104 into a UK cell (155).
- Do not treat a foreign “family status” or “carer” protected characteristic as a stacking rule.
- Do not recode Hedger v BDA (Deaf mother, hours after a newborn, sex discrimination) as a multi-disabled-household leave case.
- Do not recode Gennery, Whittaker, Gemma (hearing daughter of two Deaf parents), Tom-the-paid-carer, or Locked Out / BBC Hull patient-interpreter failures as this object.
- Do not reuse Bussey, Clinton, Graham, Rimmer, Clarke, Borys, Murphy, Bentley, Davies, Fletcher, or the EAP / OH / AtW named cases.
- Do not generalise ONS sandwich-carer 53% or Contact 62% into a Deaf household rate. Dual-generation / parent-carer exit, not this join.
- Do not print Carers Week 2025 “40% postponed an appointment” as parents missing EHCP reviews because of work. That figure is the carer’s *own* health.
- Do not print that a named employer caused a named missed family event without a source that says exactly that (157).
- Do not print “Acas has no BSL route” (110 already closed the opposite for the helpline). 151’s finding is that the *leave toolkit pages* do not specify how a BSL-first employee does the FW consultation.
- Do not treat a written form, English captions, or “put it in an email” as discharging a documented BSL-interpreter request for a negotiation meeting.
- Do not treat an Acas companion (s.10) as an interpreter.
- Do not write ERA 2025 flexible-working reasonableness as live law. ACAS places it in 2027.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment — which clock, which cap, who pays.

| Someone wants a letter / template | Use ACAS model FW letter and the GOV.UK carer’s-leave notice rule. Do not draft a “household stacking request” letter — that invents a product. A BSL booking line for the consultation is an EA s.20 ask, not a new leave right. |

| Someone wants to say AtW should pay for the child’s EHCP review | Point at 154. Different system. Care Act / Children Act assessment may fund replacement care. AtW will not. |

## What actually closes the gap (ranked)

1. **Link the live statute pages** — GOV.UK carer’s leave (the cap sentence is already written for you), dependants (the planned-appointment exclusion is already written for you), parental leave, flexible working. Carers UK explainer restates the cap. That is enough for a public door.
2. **Watch, do not FOI-invent a series.** There is no national “household-stacking leave use” dataset to request. A useful *policy* watch is the unpublished *Make Work Pay* consultation response (closed 1 Sep 2026) and ERA 2025 s.9 commencement. Put those on the existing dossier radar, not a new FOI platform.
3. **One optional FOI, only if a human wants a policy-history cell** — Department for Business and Trade / DWP: whether the 2023 Act impact assessment or the 2026 unpaid-carers review considered a per-dependant week, and what was decided. Yes/no-or-document, not “list every household.” Do not send this as a fishing expedition. Cycle 0 still uses the existing companion log.
4. **Do not FOI employers** for “how many stacked-household requests did you refuse.” That invites s.12, invents a category they do not record, and slides toward a rights-violation frame this batch rejected.
5. **A dated stub sentence** on an existing working-carers / AtW page: “Access to Work is for the employee’s own job. Carer’s Allowance is a separate benefit with an earnings limit. Neither is extra statutory leave.”
6. **A new draft** — only the one-screen chooser in “thin originals,” after sign-off. No second campaign page.

- Distinct from **144 / 145** because those ask whether an EAP *service* names a disabled family member or coordinates two needs. This batch asks what the *statute* gives the household.
- Distinct from **72–77** because CODA is a hearing child of Deaf adults, not an EHCP child of a Deaf employee plus a disabled partner.
- Distinct from **34–37 / EOL / Locked Out** because those are patient / dying-room access. 157 looked at whether the *employment* route caused a missed family decision and did not find it. Do not borrow the 80%.

- Shared facts that must **not** be reprinted as if this batch discovered them: AtW cap and 3,210 BSL customers; NRCPD headcount; sandwich-carer ONS 53%; Contact 62%; Carer’s Allowance £86.45 / £204; civil-servant AtW exclusion from 13 Nov 2025.
