## 170.1 What this does and does not lift

**Lifted, with a locator:** public copy may now say the ET awarded **£2,500** for the 16 December 1997 failure to arrange a sign interpreter; the interview was adjourned to 18 December 1997; on that day the appellant attended with Dr Sheppard, described by the EAT as “a highly competent amateur signer”; the appointment complaint and the appeal on that ground failed. Cite `[1999] UKEAT 0006_19_1101` / EAT/6/99.
