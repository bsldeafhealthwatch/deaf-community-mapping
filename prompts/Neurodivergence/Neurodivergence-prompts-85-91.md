# Prompts 85–91 — Neurodivergence Overlap (Deaf/BSL × autism, ADHD, dyslexia and related conditions), all ages

Added: 10 September 2026. Covers the intersection of Deafness/BSL use and neurodivergence across childhood, working age, and older age — sometimes referred to as "DeafDisabled" or dual/multiple needs.

Checked first: index (`prompts/SuperGrok_Research_Prompt_Pack.md`) and `SUPERGROK_PROMPTS.md`. The only prior coverage is lettered prompt B3 (Domain 6), a single unnumbered prompt asking for UK prevalence data, service gaps, and organisations serving the intersection — it was never run through Grok as a numbered batch, and no findings or review file exists anywhere in this project. This batch is not a duplicate: it supersedes B3 by giving the topic the full four-leg treatment B3 never got, and is distinct from the Deaf-older-people batch (66–71, ageing/pensions/care/loneliness with no neurodivergence angle) and the CODA batch (72–77, hearing/Deaf-of-Deaf children of Deaf adults, not neurodivergent Deaf children) because neither touches co-occurring neurodivergence at all.

Do not collapse this into AIS 34–37 (contact-method flags, not diagnostic tools), DWP/AtW/PIP 20–26 or 52–54 (Deaf-specific welfare, not the compound need), or Jobcentre 27–33. Cross-reference those batches where a figure already lives; do not re-litigate them as if new.

Anchor: three linked situations every prompt in this batch must stay honest to —

- (a) a Deaf BSL-using autistic child in mainstream or Deaf-school education whose autism assessment pathway assumes spoken/written English or lacks a BSL-fluent assessor,
- (b) a Deaf BSL-using autistic or ADHD adult applying for PIP / Access to Work / reasonable adjustments where DWP or an employer treats "Deaf" and "neurodivergent" as separate boxes rather than one compounding access need,
- (c) an older Deaf BSL-user with an undiagnosed or late-diagnosed neurodivergent profile, where diagnostic pathways built around younger people leave them without a route to assessment at all.

Proposed domain: this is Domain 6 on the original framework list, but never built out — treat this batch as finally standing it up properly, not as a new domain number.

Dossier rule: stay unbiased; follow money and fulfilment; do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. An unsourced AI claim is the lowest tier. Gaps stay gaps.

## Notes for this batch

- **Gate prompt: 85.** The definitions / prevalence / diagnostic-pathway split in 85 governs how 86–88 are read — if Grok cannot establish a UK prevalence figure or a validated BSL diagnostic tool in 85, treat that absence as the batch's likely headline finding, not a gap to paper over in the later life-stage prompts.
- **Still-alive scepticism applies to 90 especially.** A Deaf/neurodivergence joint project is exactly the kind of small, grant-funded, easily-dormant initiative this project has repeatedly found listed but inactive (see the Deaf-older and CODA reviews). Check Charity Commission filing status and last public activity, not just whether a page loads.
- **Hard-don't for this batch:** do not print any claim that a Deaf neurodivergent person has an enforceable right to a combined or BSL-specific assessment pathway, or that current pathways are unlawful, without a named statute, regulation, NICE guideline mandate, or tribunal/court judgment. The likely finding is an absence of any such pathway, not a breach of one that exists — those are different claims and must not be conflated.
- **Held-back legs: none.** Unlike some recent batches, the lived-experience prompt (91) is included in this pass rather than deferred, because B3 was never followed up at all and the domain has zero prior findings to build the legal/data layer from first. If usage constraints require trimming, drop 91 first and run it as its own follow-up (it would remain numbered 91, not renumbered).
- **Life-stage split (86, 87, 88)** follows the "all ages" framing Graeme asked for — this is a genuine sub-group split, not padding: childhood SEND/EHCP processes, working-age DWP/employment processes, and older-age adult diagnostic/Care Act processes are three different legal and institutional frameworks, and collapsing them into one prompt would lose which framework each finding actually belongs to.
- **Four nations.** England EHCP / Care Act / NICE / NHS England pathways do not stand in for Scotland, Wales, or Northern Ireland. If a finding is England-only, say so. Do not paste Scotland's tighter interpreting policy into an England cell.
- **Do not reuse Elliott.** The HMRC batch already recorded that the named PHSO/Adjudicator Elliott matter is dyslexia/ADHD, not Deaf × HMRC. Do not recruit it as a Deaf-neurodivergence case.
