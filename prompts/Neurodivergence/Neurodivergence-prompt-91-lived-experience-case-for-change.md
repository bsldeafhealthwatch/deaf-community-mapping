# Prompt 91 — Lived experience and the case for change (published testimony only)

**Master index prompt 91.** Seventh and last file in the Neurodivergence Overlap batch (prompts 85–91). Compiled 10 September 2026. Dossier rule: stay unbiased; follow money and fulfilment. An unsourced AI claim is the lowest tier. Gaps stay gaps.

**What this prompt is for.** Document, from published sources, what families and adults say happens when Deaf/BSL and neurodivergence are treated as separate boxes. Three life-stage anchors stay in separate rows. One childhood story does not stand in for working age or older age. Not a campaign brief.

**Already on file — reuse only if the source actually describes the overlap.** Francesca Bussey, Amanda Casson-Webb, PHSO Alan Graham, PHSO Mrs W, Pearl Clinton are Deaf or EOL testimony, not neurodivergence-overlap unless a cited source says so. **Hard-don't.** Do not generalise one family into a national rate. Do not print that current pathways are unlawful. The batch finding is *absence of a combined pathway*, not breach of one that exists.

## Headline

Published UK testimony at this intersection is **thin, childhood-heavy, and almost all parent-voice**. Two NDCS family stories and one Scottish placing-request judgment carry the childhood anchor. One signed-video interview carries a working-age first-person voice. **No named older-age first-person account** of late-diagnosed autism or ADHD in a Deaf BSL-user was found.

The sentence that repeats across the childhood sources is diagnostic overshadowing in primary care: needs that look like autism or ADHD are attributed to deafness until a specialist (usually **Deaf CAMHS**) unpicks them. That is the same mechanism NDCS puts on its advice page and the same mechanism *KZ* shows in the Court of Protection, except *KZ* is language-deprivation mis-labelled as learning disability in a young adult, not an older-age case.

The named **policy ask** that is actually about this group, rather than about Deafness or autism separately, is Locked Out (BSL Advisory Board, 27 Nov 2025): commission BSL-led provision for “learning disability and neurodevelopmental conditions (such as ADHD, autism)” and deliver diagnostic and post-diagnostic support in BSL. That is an ask. It is not a finding that a legal duty has been breached.

Do not stack these stories into “the community says X.” Two named mothers, one tribunal child, one YouTube interviewee and one Board report are the documented set.

## 91.1 Childhood — the documented pattern, not a rate

Kate/Xander and Leigh/Hetty (Rafael and Nat) are the two published English parent stories. Both run through **Deaf CAMHS** for the autism diagnosis. Both describe years of “it’s the deafness.” Both then fight a *second* battle for BSL in school, because an autism-unit placement without fluent BSL (the Scottish tribunal child’s situation) or a mainstream placement without a deaf peer group leaves one need sitting on top of the other.

That is consistent with prompts 85–86: NDCAMHS exists; a combined EHCP pathway does not; primary-need coding can hide the second need. It is **two families plus one judgment**. Print them as documents. Do not print “Deaf autistic children are routinely failed.”

## 91.2 Working age — one voice, no case file

William’s BSL Blethers interview is the only first-person adult account opened this pass. Diagnosis at 29, after a career of experiences blamed on deafness, and a further autism assessment still in train. It matches the 87 finding that AtW/PIP have no combined product and no named compound case. It does not fill that case-file gap. Timms grouping Deaf and neurodivergent *claimants* is useful atmosphere for a review, not testimony.

## 91.3 Older age — empty cell

No named older Deaf BSL-user describing a late autism or ADHD diagnosis, or a dementia assessment that missed lifelong neurodivergence, was found. *KZ* shows the mis-label mechanism at 20. Queen Square shows that a dementia clinic for BSL-users exists. Neither is older-age overlap testimony. Leave the cell empty.

## 91.4 The case for change that can be printed

The printable ask is the one the BSL Advisory Board already wrote down: BSL-led diagnostic and post-diagnostic support for neurodevelopmental conditions, because culture, cognition, language and behaviour cannot be assessed through an English-language tool plus an interpreter and still be valid. That is Locked Out paragraph language, dated 27 Nov 2025.

What cannot be printed from this file: a national lived-experience rate; an unlawful-pathway claim; a statement that “families say the system always chooses one need.” The documented set is smaller than that.
