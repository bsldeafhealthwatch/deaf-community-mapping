## Don't list (print near any public page)

- Do not print an official UK autism/ADHD/dyslexia **rate among Deaf BSL-users**. CRIDE 24% additional need is not condition-split and not BSL-split. NDCS “1 in 4” restates CRIDE. Locked Out “3.5% of autistic children are deaf” stays contested until the footnote is opened.
- Do not print US 1–2% ASD-in-deaf-children, IDEA “Deaf Plus,” NDIS lists, or Kentalis as UK facts.
- Do not print that a Deaf neurodivergent person has an **enforceable right** to a combined or BSL-specific assessment pathway, or that current pathways are **unlawful**. Absence of a pathway is not breach of a pathway.
- Do not print NICE CG128 / CG142 as a BSL-fluent assessor mandate. CG128 = skills or access for severe hearing impairment. CG142 = complexity flag for specialist autism-team input, not a BSL clinic.
- Do not print ADOS-2 / ADI-R / SRS-2 Deaf adaptations as a commissioned national service. They are research products. Authors: do not interpreter-mediate ADOS; Level 3 BSL is not enough.
- Do not print adult NHS or private BSL ADHD/autism pathway as if it exists. Locked Out recorded the absence; Psychiatry UK / RTC is an English-language online product, not NDCAMHS-for-adults.
- Do not print “DeafDisabled” as a NICE or GOV.UK term.
- Do not print child-interpreting as illegal (England) — that is the CODA batch rule; it is not an ND finding.
- Do not print AtW as two grants or two caps. One grant, separate tariffs, **one cap £69,260** frozen to 31 Mar 2027. No official overlap count inside the 3,210 BSL-interpreter customers.
- Do not print a combined PIP product. Timms “neurodivergent and Deaf claimants” is evidence language, not a pathway.
- Do not print Oliver McGowan or Queen Square as Deaf+autism services. McGowan = LD/autism staff training. Queen Square = acquired dementia in BSL-users, median age 71.5.
- Do not print *Oldham MBC v KZ* as an older-age case. KZ was 20. Mechanism is usable; age-band is not.
- Do not print LGSCO Brent 22 000 781 or FTS/HEC/23/0162 as an England “autism missed because Deaf” ratio. Two cases, different jurisdictions.
- Do not print Hayfield or the Deaf Academy as a national Deaf-autism service. Regional / school caseloads that *name* autism.
- Do not print BSMHD 1057135 as a current route until 2024/25 activity is opened (last dated public activity found ~May 2021).
- Do not print Katakata CIC as a national pathway. Brixton café, Active company.
- Do not print NDCS→NAS hyperlinks as a partnership or joint clinic.
- Do not generalise Xander/Kate + Rafael/Nat + William’s video into “families say the system always…”
- Do not reuse Elliott, Francesca Bussey, Amanda Casson-Webb, Thomas (MSI), or PHSO Alan Graham as overlap testimony.
- Do not paste Scotland F2F interpreting policy or FTS/HEC/23/0162 into an England cell as if it binds an ICB.
