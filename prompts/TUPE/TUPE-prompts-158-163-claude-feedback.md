## Verdict for any public page

**Curate first. Draft almost nothing.**

The one sentence a page is allowed to lead with, if any:

> A TUPE transfer does not switch off the Equality Act duty. The incoming employer must consider reasonable adjustments from day one. An Access to Work support-worker award does not move by itself — the public factsheet says contact AtW; the staff guide tells the case manager to carry a same-job takeover forward after someone phones. A workplace adjustment passport is not employee liability information.

## Don't list (print near any workplace / AtW / TUPE page)

- Do not print that a named employer’s TUPE due diligence, ELI pack, consultation format or AtW handover was unlawful or non-compliant without a named statute, regulation or judgment on those facts. There isn’t one in this batch.
- Do not print “TUPE requires X” and “in practice this is commonly omitted” as the same claim.
- Do not print an informal adjustment or a passport as a regulation-4 term. Silence on that point is the 158 headline, not a failed search.
- Do not print a statutory grace period after the transfer. There isn’t one. Sch.8 para.20 knowledge is the practical gap.
- Do not print ERA s.1 “any other benefits” as settled cover for an interpreter arrangement. Contested construction only (158).
- Do not print *Gunn* as a Deaf/BSL or informal-adjustment-transfer holding. Applicant; incoming employer status; not this object.
- Do not print “ACAS requires a BSL interpreter at every TUPE consultation.” The how-to example is accessible *documents* (159).
- Do not treat a union-rep briefing as proof that every Deaf member was consulted in an accessible format. Reg.13 is representatives-first.
- Do not treat built-in English captions, a written pack, or “the union will explain it” as discharging a documented BSL-interpreter request for the meeting itself.
- Do not recode UNISON’s 2025 welfare-reform BSL motion, BDF redundancy-consultation guidance, EHRC hybrid-working meeting advice, or Unite/JustAnswer casework threads as TUPE-consultation findings.
- Do not print “AtW continues automatically across TUPE” or “AtW always requires a brand-new application after TUPE.” Same-job takeover is a **repeat application after contact** on the staff guide; the factsheet says support-worker awards cannot transfer automatically (160).
- Do not upgrade the staff-guide sentence “TUPE obliges the new owner to take on cost-share agreements with AtW” into a tribunal holding that the award novates.
- Do not treat a service-provision change / lost retender as automatically the same box as “new owner of the business, same job.” Unpublished which box case managers use.
- Do not reprint the cap, the 3,210 BSL-interpreter count, hourly bands, or NAO/PAC waits as if they answered portability. Those live in 20 / 52–54 / 116.
- Do not treat BDF’s “support stops when the employee leaves” as a TUPE same-job finding. Leaving-job language.
- Do not print that omitting a passport from ELI is unlawful. It is not a listed particular (161).
- Do not print a passport as a legal guarantee that the incoming employer must implement the old list without a fresh reasonableness assessment.
- Do not collapse 102 (intra-employer / CS move) into 161 (legal employer changes).
- Do not paste COSOP or CS importing-manager sentences into a private-sector ELI cell.
- Do not reprint Lisa’s 2016 Civil Service blog as a TUPE example.
- Do not treat Unite’s extra-DD row as regulation 11.
- Do not paste ARD 2001/23, ADA/EEOC successor-claim liability, Australia Fair Work / EAF, CHRA, 2000/78 Art.5 or BGB § 613a into a UK cell (162).
- Do not recode US successor *claim* liability, ADA reassignment, or Irish “transfer as an accommodation” (job move inside one employer) as transfer-of-undertakings continuity.
- Do not fill the empty 163 testimony cell with a composite, with Scope leaving-job pages, or with generic “protect TUPE” outsourcing copy that never names disability or BSL.
- Do not recode *Stinton* 3200016/2018 or *Anne v GOSH* [2026] EAT 15 as transfer-continuity testimony. *Anne* is pay, not disability.
- Do not reuse Rimmer, Clarke, Bentley, Iqbal, Murphy, Davies, Khan, Fletcher, Bussey, Clinton, Graham, Borys, or Lisa 2016 as TUPE-transfer accounts.
- Do not collapse this batch into 109–114. Grievance after the transfer is already written. This batch stops at the mechanism.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment — whose purchase order, whose AtW named employer, whose data room.

## What to do with this finding

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| Someone asks “does my interpreter booking transfer?” | Split the three machines. The *agency contract* is with the old employer and is not novated by TUPE. s.20 sits on the new employer from day one if they know or ought to know. AtW hours: phone AtW; same-job takeover is written as a repeat application on the staff guide, not as an automatic event. Do not add “so they must keep the old agency.” |
| Someone asks “is it illegal that the new HR didn’t get my passport?” | “A passport is not on the ELI list. The incoming employer still has an s.20 duty from day one. You can choose to show them the document. Omitting it from the pack is not, on any holding here, a regulation 11 failure.” |
| Someone asks “was the voice-only TUPE briefing unlawful?” | ACAS says information must be accessible and names documents. Format is an s.20 question on those facts, not a listed regulation 13 particular. No judgment found. Do not add “so they are in breach.” |
| Someone wants a letter / template | Use the 46–51 tone. Ask: transfer date, old employer / new employer names, whether a union was recognised, what was requested for the briefing, what AtW element is in payment, whether the customer has contacted AtW since the transfer, whether a passport exists and was offered. Clock and grievance sit in 109–114. Do not cite a missed passport as ELI breach. |
| Someone wants a new domain page | No. Stub under Welfare & Employment. One sentence above, dated. |
| Someone wants to quote “TUPE obliges the buyer to take the AtW cost-share” as law | Don’t. That is DWP operational guidance inside the staff guide, not a judgment (160 / 158). |
