## Stablecoins and “duty of care” — what the page may and may not say

The gap is real. The legal slogan is not.

**Facts Claude can print (prompt 61):**

- Eligible stablecoins are due to be treated more like money from **6 April 2027** (individuals and trustees). CGT exemption on eligible-stablecoin disposals; interest-like returns as savings income. HMRC policy paper 13 July 2026.
- Same day: crypto loans / liquidity pools, no gain / no loss from 6 April 2027. HMRC’s own impact line: about **700,000** individuals in those arrangements.
- CARF already collecting from **1 January 2026**; first exchange reports to HMRC **Jan–May 2027**.
- HMRC crypto video is English-only (24 Sep 2024). Pay-crypto page gained **Welsh** on 29 April 2026. No BSL counterpart found. RAD does not list crypto on the HMRC topic list.
- Disclosure follow-up is published as **03000 55 22 94** (voice).

**What that is:** a communications and accessibility gap on a change that will hit a large retail cohort seven months from this note, while exchange data starts landing on HMRC’s desk in the same window. For a BSL-first reader the only doors are general Extra Support / SignVideo / RAD. That is a serious product hole. “Shocking” is a fair plain-English reaction. “Big failure of official communications” is a fair policy sentence.

**What that is not:** a ready-made common-law “duty of care to inform” finding the site should print.

| Hook | What it actually does | What it does not do |
|---|---|---|
| Common-law duty of care / negligence | Rarely how UK tax-guidance disputes are won. HMRC’s relationship with taxpayers is statutory | Do not tell a reader they can sue HMRC in negligence because there is no stablecoin BSL film |
| Equality Act 2010 s.20 / s.29 | Anticipatory reasonable-adjustment duty on a service provider. Information format can be an adjustment. Substantial-disadvantage test. Case-specific | Not “every policy paper must ship as a BSL film.” HMRC *does* offer SignVideo Extra Support and funds RAD. A claimant would have to show the *service* (understanding and meeting the new rule) put them at a substantial disadvantage and that a BSL explainer / routed Extra Support was a reasonable step that was not taken |
| Public Sector Equality Duty (s.149) | Process duty: have due regard. Useful in a complaint or a judicial-review about *how* a change was rolled out | Weak as a standalone private claim for damages |
| BSL Act 2022 reporting | Scheduled **ministerial** departments report on BSL in public communications. **HM Treasury** is in that world (five-year BSL plan, livestreamed speeches). **HMRC is a non-ministerial department** and is not the same reporting body | Do not write “HMRC is in breach of the BSL Act for this page.” Treasury reporting counts communications products; it does not create a private right to a stablecoin BSL film |
| Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations 2018 | WCAG on websites. The stablecoin draft legislation bundle already has an “accessible version” HTML of the clauses | WCAG ≠ BSL content. A page can be WCAG-compliant and still useless to a BSL-first reader |
| HMRC Charter | Statutory aspiration under CRCA 2005. “Being aware of your personal situation” / extra support. Name it in a **service complaint** | Not a duty-of-care clause |
| PHSO / Adjudicator | Service-failure and maladministration. Elliott shows PHSO will look at HMRC communication-access — and Elliott was not Deaf | No published Deaf crypto or stablecoin finding exists. Do not invent one |

**Sentence the page may use:**

> From 6 April 2027 the tax rules for some stablecoins change. HMRC has published that in English (and added Welsh on a related payment page). We have not found a British Sign Language version. If you use BSL, that is a real gap. Use SignVideo Extra Support or RAD and ask them to explain the change. This site is not saying HMRC has been found in breach of a duty of care. The Equality Act reasonable-adjustment duty is the live legal hook if the service itself is inaccessible to you.

**Sentence the page must not use:**

> HMRC has failed its duty of care to the Deaf community by not producing a stablecoin BSL film.

Leave findings and slogans off the public page. Put the gap in the dossier. If a later prompt wants a legal memo, commission it — do not let a guide page do the memo’s job.
