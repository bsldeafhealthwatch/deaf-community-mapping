# Prompt 56 — HMRC Extra Support Team: does BSL access actually reach this team, and how

**Master index prompt 56.** Second file in the HMRC and BSL/Deaf access batch (prompts 55–61). Compiled 9 September 2026. Read with prompt 55: official VRS is SignVideo Extra Support / Debt Management, not InterpretersLive. Dossier rule: stay unbiased; follow money and fulfilment. Gaps are logged as gaps.

**What this prompt is for.** Separate the Extra Support Team (EST) from general HMRC contact. A SignVideo button labelled “Extra Support” is not the same thing as a published guarantee that a BSL user lands on EST, stays flagged, and can do self-employed work (reasonable-excuse penalty, Time to Pay, MTD) in BSL.

---

## Headline

There is **no public Extra Support Team phone number**. The published routes into EST are:

1. Call an ordinary HMRC helpline (or Relay UK / textphone on that number) and tell the adviser you need extra support — they decide whether to transfer.
2. Extra Support Team **webchat**, after an eligibility screen. Webchat is written English.
3. A GOV.UK face-to-face / home-visit request form, then email.
4. The SignVideo host HMRC publishes under extra support (`hmrcextrasupport.signvideo.net`). HMRC does **not** publish what skill group that call lands on.

LITRG (updated 29 July 2026), citing HMRC, is the only source that spells out the intended phone mechanics: **warm handover** so the caller does not re-explain, and a **48-hour call-back** if no EST adviser is free. HMRC’s own Charter principles (30 July 2024) say advisers “will then transfer calls to our Extra Support Team” and that the team will “provide single points of contact”. Neither document names BSL, SignVideo, or a flag that survives to the next contact.

EST **can**, on LITRG’s HMRC-confirmed list, cancel a late-filing penalty where there is a reasonable excuse, guide a self-employed person through a straightforward return, take paper-return figures, arrange Teams or face-to-face if phone will not work, and join up with Debt Management on Time to Pay or (rarely) remission. EST **will not** complete an online return for the taxpayer. Debt is first-instance Debt Management, not EST. Making Tax Digital is **not mentioned** on the LITRG EST page.

No published FOI, PHSO, Adjudicator, or charity casework note was found that names a Deaf or DeafBlind person whose request for Extra Support went wrong (asked to use the phone anyway, told to bring a hearing relative, dropped at transfer). That absence is the finding for question (4). The nearest published accounts describe RAD’s HMRC Advice Team working *with* EST as the intended workaround, and Debt Management’s own staff manual (updated 7 September 2026) telling officers to accept a family-or-friend BSL interpreter on inbound calls.

## 56.1 Route into Extra Support for a BSL user

### What HMRC publishes

Landing page: “If you need extra support, you can get a phone or video appointment with the extra support team. Ask your adviser when you call an HMRC helpline or use the extra support team webchat service.”  
https://www.gov.uk/get-help-hmrc-extra-support

BSL is handled on a different chapter of the same guide, not on the EST contact page. That chapter lists Relay UK, textphone for some lines, webchat, SignVideo Extra Support, SignVideo Debt Management, and a face-to-face form. It does not say “this SignVideo button puts you through to Extra Support Team advisers”.  
https://www.gov.uk/get-help-hmrc-extra-support/cannot-use-telephone-different-way-to-contact

EST contact page (published 14 May 2025, last updated 19 January 2026) has **no phone number and no BSL/SignVideo mention**. Webchat requires the user to “check if you can get help from the Extra Support Team before you can contact them. If they can help you, you’ll be transferred to an adviser if one is available.” Have a National Insurance number ready. Hours: Monday–Friday 08:00–19:30, Saturday 08:00–16:00.  
https://www.gov.uk/find-hmrc-contacts/extra-support-team

More-time chapter: call the relevant contact number, “They will transfer you to HMRC’s extra support team. You may need to prove why you need more time.” Webchat is offered as the alternative. Deaf/BSL is not named on that chapter.  
https://www.gov.uk/get-help-hmrc-extra-support/if-you-need-more-time-because-of-your-circumstances

### What LITRG publishes (the only detailed routing note)

LITRG, “HMRC’s extra support team”, updated 29 July 2026:

> There is not a unique phone number for the team. You can access the extra support team via the ordinary HMRC helplines – you should tell the telephone adviser about any special circumstances that may be affecting you on either a temporary or permanent basis. … The helpline advisers decide, based on their contact with you, whether they need to hand you over to the extra support team. If so, this should be a warm handover to the extra support team so that you do not have to explain your circumstances again. We understand from HMRC that if a member of the extra support team is not available, the helpline adviser will take your details and arrange for an extra support team adviser to call you back within 48 hours.

LITRG also says webchat “can often be a quicker option than telephoning”, and that EST “will try to deal with queries over the phone as much as possible”, with face-to-face or home visit if the person finds the telephone difficult.  
https://www.litrg.org.uk/tax-nic/getting-help-hub/hmrcs-extra-support-team

A FOI to HMRC on internal transfer numbers (FOI2025/41653, 28 April 2025) is consistent with that model even though it is not EST-specific: HMRC has no internal transfer numbers; advisers pick a “telephony routing skill” and can do a “warm handover” where details are described before the call moves.  
https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/request/internal_phone_numbers_for_helpl

### What that means for a BSL-first user

Four published doors, none of them a documented “BSL queue for EST”:

| Door | Channel | Lands on EST? | Language |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary helpline + ask for extra support | Voice, or Relay UK 18001 + number, or a dedicated textphone where one exists | Only if the first adviser decides to transfer | Spoken English (Relay is typed English) |
| EST webchat | Written chat after an eligibility screen | Yes, if the screen passes and an adviser is free | Written English |
| SignVideo Extra Support host | BSL interpreter → HMRC | Unpublished. Host is labelled Extra Support on GOV.UK; skill group not published | BSL to interpreter, then spoken English to HMRC |
| Face-to-face / home visit form | Online form, then email | Request is for EST | Written English form. Current official start URL: https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/guidance/request-a-face-to-face-meeting-with-hmrc/start/introduction . LITRG still cites an older `hmrc.gov.uk/tools/visits/visit-request.htm` path |

A fifth door sits outside HMRC: RAD’s HMRC Advice Team (prompt 55). Charity Today, 23 September 2024: “The team at RAD work closely with HMRC’s Extra Support Team who are able to liaise with all lines of HMRC business internally and externally to address the client’s needs.” That is RAD-to-EST, not taxpayer-to-EST in BSL.  
https://www.charitytoday.co.uk/hmrc-celebrates-royal-association-for-deaf-peoples-support-for-deaf-taxpayers/

**Not published:** whether the SignVideo Extra Support interpreter is briefed to ask for EST; whether a Relay UK call to Income Tax 0300 200 3300 is treated as an extra-support flag; whether the 48-hour call-back is offered in BSL, Relay, or voice-to-a-hearing-relative.

## 56.2 Priority, flagging, not re-explaining

HMRC Charter principles of support, updated 30 July 2024:

> We will monitor calls and correspondence with HMRC to identify customers who may need extra help. This could include difficulties caused by their confidence, access, emotional state, mental health, capability, difficulties in understanding what they need to do, and disability. We will also ask customers to tell us if they need extra support.
>
> Our advisers will then transfer calls to our Extra Support Team who have appropriate skills, knowledge and empathy to provide support and reasonable adjustments to meet customers’ needs. They will also provide single points of contact to resolve difficult queries.

https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/hmrc-charter/hmrcs-principles-of-support-for-customers-who-need-extra-help--3

That is a transfer-and-single-point commitment. It is **not** a published BSL flag, a “do not make this person repeat themselves on the next letter”, or a priority queue. BSL is not named.

LITRG’s “warm handover / 48-hour call-back” note is the nearest operational statement. It is LITRG reporting what HMRC told them, not a GOV.UK page a taxpayer can cite back to an adviser.

Debt Management staff manual DMBM510500 (updated **7 September 2026**) tells officers taking inbound Text Relay or BSL-interpreter calls to note the preferred method and check notes before an outbound call. That is a flagging instruction for Debt Management, not a published EST customer-facing promise.  
https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/debt-management-and-banking/dmbm510500

No HMRC page was found that says “BSL / communication-access requests are prioritised”.

## 56.3 What Extra Support can and cannot do for a self-employed / freelance person

### Scope lists do not match

GOV.UK EST webchat list (19 January 2026): Child Benefit; CIS; Corporation Tax; Horizon Shortfall Scheme; National Insurance; PAYE; Self Assessment; Simple Assessment; tax for employers including employers’ PAYE. Contact another helpline if the question is about something else, or if you do not need extra support because of condition or circumstances.

LITRG (29 July 2026) adds income tax, legacy tax credits, inheritance tax and VAT, and says the GOV.UK list is the **webchat** list only — phone and face-to-face can go further. Treat the two lists as contested on VAT and tax credits via webchat.

### Self-employed tasks, as documented

| Task | Documented EST role | Channel documented | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late-filing penalty where there is a reasonable excuse | Yes. LITRG Joyce example: EST “help cancel the late filing penalties … as she clearly had a ‘reasonable excuse’”. Also: if paper deadline missed and the person cannot file online, EST “will consider whether it is possible to cancel any late filing penalties”. | Phone / unspecified. Joyce is not a BSL case | LITRG EST page, 29 Jul 2026 |
| Help completing a straightforward Self Assessment return | Paper: EST can take the numbers, put them in the boxes, send the return back to check and sign. Online: EST will **not** enter figures; will try to guide the taxpayer; Teams or face-to-face if phone will not work. “At no point will HMRC complete an online return for the taxpayer, no matter how simple.” | Phone first; Teams or F2F fallback | Same, HMRC-confirmed to LITRG |
| Cancel an unnecessary return / take the person out of Self Assessment | Yes, in the Joyce example (stopped trading) | Unspecified | Same |
| Tax code, employment history, account balance, delayed P800 causing hardship | Yes | Unspecified | Same |
| Time to Pay / payment plan | First door is **Debt Management**, not EST. LITRG: contact Debt Management on the letter or the payment-problems numbers; explain circumstances; they may help themselves or pass to EST. Online Time to Pay for SA ≤ £30,000 is a self-serve digital product with a Relay line of its own (18001 then 0345 300 3900 on that page — number looks stale relative to current tax-credits numbering; do not treat as an EST number) | Debt Management voice / online; EST only if referred | LITRG EST page; https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/pay-what-you-owe-in-instalments |
| Remission (stop pursuing the debt) | Rare; EST can be asked to take the debt aspect back. Most common for elderly / sick / long-term unemployed with no assets | Unspecified | LITRG EST page |
| Making Tax Digital quarterly submissions | **Not mentioned** on the LITRG EST page or the GOV.UK EST contact page. RAD runs MTD workshops for Deaf clients. MTD for VAT accessibility statement says HMRC “can provide a British Sign Language (BSL) interpreter, or you can arrange a visit from an HMRC advisor to help you complete the service” and points at extra support — that is the VAT *service* statement, not an EST product description | No EST-via-BSL MTD walkthrough found | RAD events list; https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/accessibility-statement/vat-through-software |
| Complex / long-running dispute, sophisticated calculations, loan charge | LITRG: EST **may not** be able to help; handover to TaxAid and other VCS | Phone / VCS | LITRG EST page |

Reasonable-excuse law itself is not EST-specific. Compliance Handbook CH160900 says the person must show a reasonable excuse and that if they appear to have difficulty communicating “you may need to consider referring them to the Extra Support Team”. Communication difficulty is a *referral trigger*, not a listed example of reasonable excuse.  
https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/compliance-handbook/ch160900

### Is any of this documented as available in BSL / textphone / webchat rather than phone?

- **Webchat:** yes, for the GOV.UK topic list, in written English, after a screen. Not BSL.
- **Relay / textphone:** yes as a way to reach the *first* helpline. Not documented as an EST-native channel.
- **SignVideo Extra Support:** published as a way to contact HMRC, not as a way to file a reasonable-excuse appeal or set up Time to Pay in BSL.
- **Teams / face-to-face:** documented as the fallback when an online return cannot be guided by phone. BSL at that appointment is not specified on the EST pages.
- **Phone-first default:** LITRG states it in terms. A BSL-first freelance person is being asked to enter a phone-shaped process unless they use SignVideo, RAD, or the visit form.

## 56.4 When extra-support access goes wrong — published Deaf / DeafBlind accounts

**No published account found** in this pass that names or clearly describes a Deaf or DeafBlind taxpayer whose Extra Support request failed in the ways the prompt lists (delay, told to use the phone anyway, told to bring a hearing relative). Searched: LITRG EST and additional-needs pages; RAD HMRC pages; Charity Today RAD/HMRC piece; WhatDoTheyKnow HMRC extra-support + BSL/Deaf; PHSO-style queries mixed into the same strings. That is a genuine public-record gap, not a thin source to pad.

What exists instead:

- **RAD as the working bypass.** Charity Today 23 September 2024 and HMRC’s 9 May 2026 X post present RAD’s HMRC Advice Team as the BSL layer that then talks to EST. That is evidence of a designed workaround, not evidence of how often the official doors fail.
- **Family member as interpreter is an official option, including for BSL on debt calls.** Extra-support “another language” chapter: friend or family member over 16, same room, or HMRC “may also be able to organise an interpreter”. Debt Management DMBM510500 (7 September 2026) tells staff to accept a family-or-friend BSL interpreter on inbound calls, make them vocalise both sides for the recording, and note name, address, relationship and any professional-body membership. That is the opposite of a published “do not use a hearing relative” safeguard. It is also not an Extra Support Team instruction — it is Debt Management.
- **LITRG additional-needs page** lists Relay, webchat and InterpretersLive (stale supplier name — see prompt 55) plus RAD. It does not collect failure cases.
- **Speech-recognition barrier on ordinary helplines** is documented by LITRG for people who need a spoken-language interpreter: the IVR may tell you to call back with a friend or family member. A BSL user on Relay or SignVideo is in a different pipe, but anyone forced onto a voice helpline first hits that IVR. Not a named Deaf case.

Prompt 57 is the place to press Adjudicator / PHSO named findings. Do not treat this gap as “no one has a problem”. Treat it as “no one has published a named Extra Support communication-access finding that can be cited the way Alan Graham is cited for NHS.”

## What prompt 57 should not assume

Do not write “EST is reachable in BSL via SignVideo” as a verified customer journey. Write: HMRC publishes a SignVideo host under extra support; whether that host is EST is unpublished; the documented EST doors are ordinary helpline transfer, English webchat, and a visit form; RAD is the published BSL advice layer that then talks to EST.

Do not invent a named Extra Support communication-access ombudsman case. Prompt 57 should look again at Adjudicator / PHSO and record another absence if that is what the record shows.
