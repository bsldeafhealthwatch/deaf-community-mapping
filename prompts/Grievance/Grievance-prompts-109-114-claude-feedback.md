## Don't list (print near any grievance / tribunal page)

- Do not print that a missed internal SLA, a refused adjustment, or Acas’s process is automatically unlawful, non-compliant, or discrimination. Reasonableness is fact-specific (109 gate; batch hard-don't).
- Do not print that a grievance pauses the EA clock. It does not. Only Acas notification pauses it (s.140B / ERA s.207B; *Luton BC v Haque*; *Raison* [2025] EAT 86).
- Do not print the pre-1-Oct-2026 three-month-less-one-day window as if it still governs a claim whose time *starts* on or after 1 October 2026. Conversely, do not apply the six-month window to a claim that already started.
- Do not print NI as Acas. NI is the Labour Relations Agency (1 month + 14 days). TULRCA s.207A Code uplift is a remedy, not permission to sue.
- Do not print “Acas has no BSL route.” Helpline InterpretersLive! and the EC declaration list exist. The **gap** is fulfilment method inside the conciliator conversation, and DeafBlind unnamed on Acas pages.
- Do not print HMCTS BSL as automatic. It is request-based. *M Bentley v Ealing* is one documented admin miss that was then relisted — not “HMCTS never provides BSL.”
- Do not print legal aid as ET representation as of right. LASPO left Legal Help / CLA for discrimination advice. Representation at the ET generally needs Exceptional Case Funding (s.10). LAG: generally not granted.
- Do not print EASS as a representative. EASS says it cannot give legal advice or representation. Convo + RAD is a **contact** product.
- Do not print FRU as a national service. London and the South East; volunteer not guaranteed; hearings over three days are hard to staff.
- Do not confuse the British **Dietetic** Association’s union-legal page with the British Deaf Association. BDA / RNID / SignHealth / Sense are not ET-representation products on the 111 pass.
- Do not print an ET issue fee as live. *Unison* [2017] UKSC 51 quashed fees. The 2024 £55 consultation is not a live fee on the 111 pass.
- Do not file **Rimmer** or **Clarke** as employer-grievance or tribunal-*hearing* failures. Rimmer is a Jobcentre service claim that happened to be heard in the ET. Three named interpreters were at that hearing.
- Do not file **Iqbal** as a hearing-access failure. She was represented; the BSL-hours claim was dismissed on the merits.
- Do not file **Elliott** (dyslexia/ADHD), **Murphy** (recruitment 107), **Davies / Duggal / Khan** (HR-SLA, not Deaf), **Bussey / Casson-Webb** (wrong domain), or **Little Mix / Reynolds** (county-court concert).
- Do not conflate the two Bentleys (see above).
- Do not print *Strutt* as “the tribunal found the grievance inaccessible.” UD succeeded; the disability-discrimination / BSL-adjustment head was **dismissed**.
- Do not generalise Strutt + MacLennan + C Bentley + one JustAnswer thread into “Deaf grievances are inaccessible.” 113 is thin on purpose.
- Do not promote Navaratnarajah / Unite JustAnswer posts to a reported judgment. Named, forum-sourced. The useful grain: he said Acas *did* provide BSL and could not give legal advice — which matches 110–111.
- Do not print a DeafBlind-claimant ET or a DeafBlind Acas tactile route. Neither was found. Sense as *respondent* is not a DeafBlind-claimant series (*Vickers* is eczema/allergies).
- Do not print EEOC mediation as the UK model. It is voluntary inside an agency investigation. Acas *notification* is a jurisdictional precondition. Do not paste the EEOC 84-day median or Charter s.14 into a UK cell (112).
- Do not print “ASL / Auslan as standard without request” for US / Australia conciliation. Those systems are request-plus-notice too. CRTC 2025-95 tactile language is a Canadian *telecoms hearing* guide, not CHRC employment mediation.
- Do not print a published-case win-rate. Most claims settle or withdraw; 114 is a named slice.
- Do not paste DWP complaints (prompt 33) into an employer-grievance cell.
- Do not use this batch as advice on Graeme’s personal thread.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment — who books the interpreter, who pays the advocate, whose clock is running.

## What to do with this finding

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| Someone asks “what do I do now the ticket was missed?” | Sequence only: grievance (Acas Code) → notify Acas → ET1. State the clock. Do not promise an interpreter will be in the grievance room. Employer duty (s.20 + Code Step 4 / companion ≠ interpreter) is not the same product as HMCTS BSL. |
| Someone asks “does the grievance buy me time?” | No. Notify Acas. |

| Someone wants a letter / template | Existing templates batch (46–51) tone. Ask: date of the act, date Acas was notified, who the respondents are (employer *and* principal if agency — s.41(4)), whether BSL was declared. Do not cite a missed SLA as the cause of action. |
