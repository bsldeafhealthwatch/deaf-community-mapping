## Don't list (print near any grievance / tribunal page)

- Do not print that a missed internal SLA, a refused adjustment, or Acas's process is automatically unlawful, non-compliant, or discrimination. Reasonableness is fact-specific.
- Do not print that a grievance pauses the EA clock. It does not — confirmed live on acas.org.uk. Only Acas notification pauses it (s.140B / ERA s.207B; *Luton BC v Haque*; *Raison* [2025] EAT 86).
- Do not print the pre-1-Oct-2026 three-month-less-one-day window as if it governs a claim whose time *starts* on or after 1 October 2026. Conversely, do not apply the six-month window to a claim that already started. Confirmed live: the change is conditional on the start date, not a blanket switch on the calendar date.
- Do not print Northern Ireland as Acas territory. NI is the Labour Relations Agency (1 month + 14 days).
- Do not print "Acas has no BSL route." Helpline InterpretersLive! and the EC declaration list — including "a British Sign Language interpreter" as a named, literal option — are confirmed live. The gap is the fulfilment method inside the conciliator conversation itself, and DeafBlind is unnamed on Acas's pages.
- Do not print HMCTS BSL as automatic. It is request-based. *M Bentley v Ealing* is one documented admin miss that was then relisted — not "HMCTS never provides BSL."
- Do not print legal aid as ET representation as of right. LASPO left Legal Help / CLA for discrimination advice. Representation at the ET generally needs Exceptional Case Funding (s.10), which LAG describes as generally not granted.
- Do not print EASS as a representative body. EASS states it cannot give legal advice or representation.
- Do not print FRU as a national service. London and the South East only; a volunteer is not guaranteed.
- Do not confuse the British Dietetic Association's union-legal page with the British Deaf Association.
- Do not print an ET issue fee as currently live. *Unison* [2017] UKSC 51 quashed the 2013 fee. The 2024 consultation on a £55 fee had not resulted in a live fee as of this pass.
- Do not file **Rimmer** or **Clarke** as employer-grievance or tribunal-hearing failures — Rimmer is a Jobcentre service claim.
- Do not file **Iqbal** as a hearing-access failure — she was represented and lost on the merits.
- Do not file Elliott, Murphy, Davies/Duggal/Khan, Bussey/Casson-Webb, or Little Mix/Reynolds into this cell — wrong domains.
- Do not conflate the two Bentleys (*M Bentley v Ealing* vs *C Bentley v Oaks Park*).
- Do not print *Strutt* as "the tribunal found the grievance inaccessible" — unfair dismissal succeeded; the BSL-adjustment/disability-discrimination head was dismissed.
- Do not generalise *Strutt* + *MacLennan* + *C Bentley* + one JustAnswer thread into "Deaf grievances are inaccessible." The lived-experience file (113) is thin by design, not by oversight.
- Do not promote the Navaratnarajah/Unite JustAnswer forum posts to the status of a reported judgment.
- Do not print a DeafBlind-claimant ET judgment or a DeafBlind Acas tactile route — neither was found. Sense as *respondent* (*Vickers*, *Prime*) is not a DeafBlind-claimant series.
- Do not print EEOC mediation as the UK model, or paste the EEOC 84-day median or Canadian Charter s.14 into a UK cell — international context only.
- Do not print a published-case win-rate. Most claims settle or withdraw; 114 is a named, non-representative slice.
- Do not use this batch as advice on Graeme's own personal circumstances.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment — who books the interpreter, who pays the advocate, whose clock is running.

## What to do with this finding

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| Someone asks "what do I do now the ticket was missed?" | Sequence only: grievance (Acas Code) → notify Acas → ET1. State the clock. Do not promise an interpreter will be in the grievance room. |
| Someone asks "does the grievance buy me time?" | No. Notify Acas regardless of where the grievance has got to. |
| Someone asks "will Acas sign with me?" | Acas publishes a BSL declaration option and a helpline VRS product (InterpretersLive!). How the conciliator conversation itself is delivered after the box is ticked is unpublished — ask in writing at notification and keep the acknowledgement. |

| Someone wants a letter / template | Use the existing templates batch (46–51) tone. Capture: date of the act, date Acas was notified, who the respondents are (employer *and* principal if agency, per s.41(4)), whether BSL was declared. Do not cite a missed SLA as the cause of action. |

| Someone wants to lead with Rimmer | Don't, on an employer page — he is the JCP comparator. Use *Thatcher* / *C Bentley* / *Strutt* (with the split result) for workplace process instead. |
