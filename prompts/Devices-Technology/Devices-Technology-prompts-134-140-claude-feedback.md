## Don't list (print near any workplace-AI / AT page)

- Do not print that offering AI technology instead of a human interpreter is lawful or unlawful, or that a named employer’s practice is discriminatory, without a named statute, regulation, or tribunal/court judgment.
- Do not print Domain 14’s NHS / patient-facing findings (SignGPT £8.4m, Trust avatar pilots, AIS) as an employment claim. Different actor, different legal framework.
- Do not print the all-customer Support Worker / SAE split as the Deaf/HoH split. The join is unpublished (prompt 134). HL3809: SAE items are free-text; a “list the items” FOI hits s.12.
- Do not print an official AtW shift from interpreters to AI captioning. No official series says that. RNID 18% is **award-shortfall coping**, not a DWP policy change.
- Do not print RNID’s £69,620. Official cap is **£69,260**.
- Do not treat built-in English captions, live captions that are not saved, or Copilot recap as discharging a requested BSL interpreter.
- Do not print “Teams captions are on by default.” They are not. Do not print the explicit-consent recording gate as on by default. It is Off.
- Do not print a named UK employer as having piloted or procured an AI signing avatar as a substitute for staff-interpreter hours. Prompt 136: not found. TfL / Signapse / SWR are **passenger-info**.
- Do not print WFD/WASLI 2018 or De Meulder as an employment-law conclusion. Public-authority / research-governance texts.
- Do not print CAN-ASC-6.2 (“human alternative at any time”), EU Parliament A10-0244/2025 §20, EEOC 2023 menu items, or Australia EAF into a UK cell. Prompt 138 is international only.
- Do not print that a colleague who objects to an AI notetaker is acting unlawfully, or that the disabled employee may insist on the bot as of right. ICO Art. 21 and EA s.20 both apply; **no official source ranks them** (prompt 137).
- Do not print “consent at the start of the call” as the ICO-preferred basis. ICO: consent is rarely valid in employment.
- Do not print that every voice file is Article 9 biometric data. Voice is personal data; special-category biometric only if used to uniquely identify.
- Do not file Philip Maguire / Microsoft Teams “game changer” as a UK civil-service case. EMEA feature; likely Irish Department of Finance (prompt 139).
- Do not reuse Bussey, Clinton, Graham, Rimmer, Clarke, Borys or Murphy as workplace-AI testimony.
- Do not generalise two RNID survey quotes into a national employer-substitution rate.
- Do not print that AI has, or has not, replaced workplace interpreters as a national fact. Prompt 140: no hours series. AtW BSL-interpreter customers and spend **rose** through 2024/25. NUBSLI still says shortage.
- Do not treat a vendor “solves interpreter scarcity” line as workforce-impact evidence.
- Do not repeat prompt 129 (MAR / SYSC walls) inside a general-meeting GDPR paragraph.
- Do not collapse 135 (captions off) into 93 (OH) or 137 (saved transcript / recap). Persistence is a different processing fact.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment — whose tenant, whose AtW element, whose booking diary.
