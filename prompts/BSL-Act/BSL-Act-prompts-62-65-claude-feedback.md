## Verdict for any public page

**Print the legal fact. Do not print a scandal.**

Safe three-line block for section 2 of the HMRC brief (replace the first-pass version with this):

> The British Sign Language Act 2022 requires listed **ministerial** departments to report on BSL in their public communications. HMRC is a non-ministerial department. It is not on the Schedule. HM Treasury is. The Act’s own amendment power (section 2(9)) can only follow machinery-of-government changes; the 2022 Delegated Powers Memorandum says it “could not be exercised to add a non-ministerial department.” Nobody in Hansard, and no published BDA / RNID / SignHealth / Advisory Board paper located to September 2026, asked for HMRC to be added. HMRC still runs SignVideo Extra Support and Debt Management, and RAD tax advice, under the Equality Act and its Charter — not under the 2022 reporting duty. Closing the *reporting* gap would need primary legislation. It would not, by itself, create a new contact-centre product.

That is the whole finding. Stop there on a public page.

## Don’t list (print near any Act / HMRC sentence)

- Do not write “HMRC is exempt from the BSL Act.” It was never in scope. Exempt implies a carve-out after a debate. There was no debate.
- Do not write “Parliament refused to include HMRC.” Refusal needs a vote. There was no amendment and no division.
- Do not write “campaigners were silenced” or “the gap was hidden.” The Delegated Powers Memorandum, the Explanatory Notes and section 2(8) state the limit in plain words. Campaign energy went to “no enforceable right” (McWhinney, Feb 2022) and “covered departments produced nothing” (DNS, Aug 2023). Those are different criticisms. Do not conflate them with this one.
- Do not write “Treasury reports for HMRC.” HMT’s 5-year BSL plan is Treasury livestreams and GCS guidance. It does not describe Extra Support SignVideo as an Act deliverable.
- Do not write “adding HMRC to the Schedule would give Deaf people a right to a BSL interpreter at HMRC.” Section 2 is a *reporting* duty on public announcements, consultations, strategies, press conferences, social media. The reports themselves say operational reasonable adjustments are out of scope. Extra Support already exists without the Act.
- Do not write “Companies House / DVLA prove the parent department’s duty extends down.” Their VRS products do not cite the Act. Companies House SignVideo went live **1 February 2022**, before Royal Assent.
- Do not treat the Wales Act 2026 or the NI Act 2026 as Westminster amendments. They list *devolved* public bodies. Isherwood said the Wales Bill fills the UK Act’s silence on devolved services, not HMRC.
- Do not caption McWhinney 2022 as an HMRC-exclusion campaign. He used “20 of its departments” as evidence the Bill was thin.
- Do not caption the 2023 “betrayal” piece as an NMD story. The target was 11 of 20 *listed* departments producing zero BSL. Treasury was named as a covered department that did nothing. HMRC was not mentioned.
- Do not hold the page out as a drafting service for a new Bill.
- Do not print “duty of care breach” because HMRC is off the Schedule (same rule as prompt 61 on the missing stablecoin film).

## What actually closes the gap

Ranked by what the instrument can really do. Sell each layer as itself.

### 1. Primary legislation — the only way to change who is listed

Amend section 2(8) and the Schedule, or replace section 2(9) so regulations can add a non-ministerial department.

- Vehicle: a **government bill**. A ballot Private Member’s Bill that tried to impose new duties on HMRC would hit the same no-new-public-expenditure constraint that stripped the 2021 draft of its Council and public-bodies clauses (prompt 62).
- Owner today: Secretary of State for Work and Pensions (the Act sits with DWP / Office for Equality and Opportunity). HMRC policy sits with Treasury ministers. Any clause that named HMRC would need both.
- What it would buy: HMRC (or “non-ministerial departments listed in a new Schedule”) would have to *report* BSL use in public communications. It would not create an individual right, a VRS SLA, or a crypto BSL film.
- Cost of overselling: readers will think a Schedule amendment fixes Extra Support routing, MTD, Trusted Helper and CARF letters. It does not.

### 2. Things that improve Deaf contact *without* amending the Act

These are the live levers. They are already how HMRC, Companies House and DVLA got VRS.

| Lever | Who | What it can do | What it cannot do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equality Act s.20 reasonable adjustment + Charter complaint | The person, via Extra Support then `gov.uk/complain-about-hmrc` then Adjudicator then PHSO via MP | Force a specific adjustment on a live case | Change the Schedule |
| Extra Support / SignVideo hosts + RAD | HMRC operational | The actual door today | Does not cover SA/VAT/PAYE as separate one-click hosts (prompt 55) |
| HMT 5-year BSL plan, next refresh | Treasury comms | Could *name* HMRC Extra Support as a case study and start counting it | Still not a statutory duty on HMRC |
| HMRC extra-support guide + crypto pages | HMRC content | Add BSL / SignVideo / Relay UK lines to the pages that currently lack them (disclosure 03000 55 22 94 is voice-only — prompt 61) | Not an Act output |
| GCS / GDS accessibility rules | Cabinet Office / DSIT | WCAG + Service Standard already bind HMRC digital | Do not create a reporting line in the BSL annual report |
| MoJ-style agency annex in a parent plan | HMT, AGO, DfT, DBT, DfE, DHSC | Soft cascade: “our NMD/agency will publish X” | Optional. MoJ did this for films, not VRS |

**Best near-term product for this site:** keep pointing at SignVideo Extra Support and RAD. Add the legal footnote above. Do not open a “campaign for a new BSL Bill” page unless a named sponsor exists.

### 3. Parliamentary and Board moves that cost little and test demand

None of these amend the Act. They create a paper trail the 2022–26 record lacks.

1. **One written question**, not three. Draft only:

   > To ask the Secretary of State for Work and Pensions, whether she has considered using, or amending, section 2 of the British Sign Language Act 2022 so that non-ministerial departments including HM Revenue and Customs report on the use of British Sign Language in their public communications.

   Table it at DWP (Timms / successor), not at HMRC. A Treasury PQ about “public bodies and AI BSL” already exists (Craft 91071) and did not open this door.

2. **One letter to the BSL Advisory Board co-chairs** (new cohort from December 2025) asking whether a published recommendation on NMDs is in scope under the current terms of reference. The published ToR says “ministerial departments.” If they say no, that is the next footnote. If they say yes, that is the first document prompt 63 could not find.

3. **Do not table an EDM.** Sign Language Week EDMs already exist and do not move the Schedule. An EDM that names HMRC without a Bill behind it is decoration.

4. **Do not FOI “why was HMRC excluded.”** The answer is in the Delegated Powers Memorandum. An FOI to the Cabinet Office BSL Act team asking *whether any department or Board paper since 2022 proposed adding NMDs* is the only FOI that would add a fact. Everything else is already on GOV.UK.

### 4. What not to build

- A “BSL Act Compliance Monitor” scorecard that marks HMRC non-compliant. HMRC cannot be non-compliant with a duty it does not have. Prompt 42 already killed official-sounding product names and volunteer legal conclusions.
- A petition to “give HMRC a BSL duty” that does not distinguish reporting from contact-centre VRS.
- A cloned Wales/NI public-bodies list dropped onto 2022 c. 34. Different legislatures, different competence.

## Paste-ready update for `claude/hmrc-bsl-access-brief.md` section 2

Replace the first-pass “is HMRC covered?” paragraph with:

**Covered by the BSL Act 2022?** No. Section 2(8) confines “relevant government department” to ministerial departments listed in the Schedule. HMRC is one of 20 non-ministerial departments. HM Treasury is listed; HMRC is not. The section 2(9) regulation power is limited to machinery-of-government changes (SI 2023/424, SI 2024/991). The Delegated Powers Memorandum, 22 March 2022, states the power cannot add a non-ministerial department. Hansard 2021–22 contains no amendment and no speech proposing HMRC. No published BDA, RNID, SignHealth or Advisory Board recommendation 2021–September 2026 asks for the Schedule to be extended (Grok 62–65, 9 September 2026). HMRC’s live BSL doors are Equality Act / Charter products: SignVideo Extra Support and Debt Management, RAD bookable advice, Relay UK (Grok 55). A future Schedule amendment would create a reporting line, not a new helpline.

**Sources to cite, not to paraphrase into slogans:** 2022 c. 34 s.2(8)–(9); DPM 22 March 2022; first–fourth BSL reports’ standing “ALBs not in scope / encouraged” line; HMT 5-year plan 28 August 2025.

## One paragraph if a journalist asks “so what do you want?”

> We want HMRC’s existing SignVideo and RAD doors to be findable on every tax line that still publishes a voice number only — starting with the crypto disclosure helpline — and we want the annual BSL report to stop implying that “government” is covering tax when the listed body is Treasury comms.
