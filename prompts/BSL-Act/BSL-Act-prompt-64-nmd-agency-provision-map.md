# Prompt 64 — Mapping non-ministerial departments and executive agencies against published Deaf/BSL provision

**Master index prompt 64.** Third file in the BSL Act 2022 scope-gap batch (prompts 62–65). Compiled 9 September 2026. Dossier rule: stay unbiased; follow money and fulfilment; do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. An unsourced AI claim is the lowest tier. A body that has published nothing findable is a finding, not a failed search.

**What this prompt is for.** Compare what eight high-stakes bodies actually publish for BSL users against the Act’s ministerial Schedule. Six are non-ministerial departments (out of the 20 currently listed on GOV.UK/government/organisations). Two are executive agencies of Schedule parents, included to test whether a parent’s BSL Act duty reaches the agency in any documented way. Prompt 62 is the Hansard answer. Prompt 63 is the campaign-archive answer. Prompt 65 is the live reform landscape.

**Method.** Each body’s own contact / extra-support / accessibility pages; SignVideo SignDirectory (live listing includes HMRC Extra Support, HMRC Debt Management, Companies House; does not list the other six in the directory extract used on this pass); InterpretersLive / Convo public listings where they appear; parent-department 5-year BSL plans published July 2025; Contracts Finder where a VRS award is public. Lived-experience / FOI / casework notes are recorded only where a named source exists.

## Headline

Provision does **not** follow the Act. It follows whoever bought a video-relay contract.

- **Two of eight bodies publish a live BSL video-relay contact channel:** HMRC (SignVideo, Extra Support + Debt Management only) and Companies House (SignVideo, whole contact centre, since 1 February 2022).
- **One more has a documented VRS contract:** DVLA with DA Languages / Sign Solutions InterpretersLive!, live from November 2023, usage “higher than expected,” re-procurement on Contracts Finder. That is an agency product. It is not described as discharging DfT’s BSL Act duty.
- **CPS names BSL** in its Accessible Communication Public Statement and will book a registered interpreter or lipspeaker for court witnesses and pre-arranged office visits. It does **not** publish a public VRS button for contacting the Service.
- **Land Registry, Charity Commission, Ofsted and the Food Standards Agency** publish no BSL video-relay channel and no BSL-named contact line located on this pass. Their pages are phone + web form + (sometimes) generic “accessible format on request.” That absence is the datum.
- **Parent-department 5-year BSL plans do not pull these bodies in.** HMT’s plan is Treasury communications, not HMRC extra support. AGO’s plan is AGO YouTube and intranet, not CPS charging decisions. MoJ’s plan *does* list its own agencies (HMCTS, HMPPS, OPG, LAA, CAFCASS) — the exception that shows the pattern is optional. Companies House’s SignVideo page never mentions the BSL Act or DBT. It went live in February 2022, two months before Royal Assent, as an operational accessibility product.

The informal-fix hypothesis in the batch notes therefore only half-stands. Companies House and DVLA *do* have BSL contact channels their parents lack a duty to report. Those channels look like Equality Act / customer-service decisions, not Act-reporting cascade. Formalising the Schedule would still be the only way to make the duty match the contact.

## 64.1 How to read the comparison

Four columns the brief asked for, plus type and Act status:

1. **VRS / equivalent published?** Formal page + working host; ad hoc (“we can arrange an interpreter if you write in”); or nothing found.
2. **BSL named** in a charter, accessibility statement or equivalent, distinct from “reasonable adjustments” / “sensory disability” / WCAG.
3. **For Companies House and DVLA only:** is the published BSL product described as, or counted under, the parent department’s BSL Act duty?
4. **Documented Deaf/BSL contact experience** — case, FOI, charity note. No reliable figure found is left as that.

Relay UK is the national text-relay overlay. It is not a BSL channel. Bodies that only have Relay UK (or nothing) are not “BSL-accessible” for the purpose of this table.

## 64.3 Body-by-body notes

### HMRC — the best-documented NMD, still not on the Act

Full channel map is prompt 55 (9 September 2026). Two SignVideo hosts, RAD Calendly advice, Relay UK, incomplete textphone coverage. The product is real. The Act does not cover it. HMT’s 5-year plan talks about Treasury livestreams and Barefoot / Sign Solutions for *departmental* comms. That is a different pile of work from a Deaf self-employed person trying to reach Self Assessment.

### HM Land Registry — high-stakes, no BSL contact page found

Buying, selling and proving title are exactly the interactions the batch brief flagged. The published contact stack is form-first, phone-second, Friday-phone-often-off. Accessibility statements for the contact forms are model WCAG text. BSL is not in that model text. Relay UK would work on 0300 006 0411 if a user knows to dial 18001 first; Land Registry does not tell them to.

### CPS — BSL in court, not on the switchboard

The Accessible Communication Public Statement is the strongest BSL-named document in the six-NMD set after HMRC:

> “For people who are deaf or have a hearing impairment the CPS will arrange the use of a registered British Sign Language (BSL) Interpreter or a Lipspeaker (for those who do not know or use BSL).”

That duty sits on the prosecution-witness / meeting side. The accessibility statement’s contact block is text relay plus “arrange a BSL interpreter” if you warn them before an office visit. There is no SignVideo/Convo/InterpretersLive CPS directory entry located. A Deaf defendant or a Deaf person trying to *ask the CPS a question* is in a different queue from a Deaf witness already inside a case.

AGO is on the Schedule. AGO’s 5-year plan does not mention CPS.

### Charity Commission, Ofsted, FSA — nothing findable

Three regulators a Deaf person might need in a hurry (register or report a charity; complain about a school; report a food-safety incident). Published contact is phone + form. No VRS. No BSL-named charter paragraph located. Ofsted’s complaints line offers an unspecified “accessibility support” option — that is not evidence of BSL. Absence on these three is the cleanest illustration of the Schedule cut: the Act produces annual counts of ministerial press-conference films; it produces no reporting hook that would make these three pages look like HMRC’s extra-support guide.

### Companies House — the informal-fix exhibit

GOV.UK, 1 February 2022, never updated according to the page’s own history:

> “Our video relay service allows Deaf BSL users to call Companies House through a team of SignVideo interpreters.”

Host: `https://companieshouse.signvideo.net`. On SignVideo’s public directory next to HMRC. Hours match the contact centre. The page does not mention the BSL Act, DBT, machinery of government, or a reporting duty. Timing is the tell: it went live *before* Royal Assent. This is an Equality Act / customer-service build, not an Act cascade. That still matters for the reform ask in prompt 65 — it shows an NMD-adjacent agency can stand up VRS without being on the Schedule, which is an argument for *and* against extension. For: the need is already recognised operationally. Against: the Act is not what produced it, so adding Companies House to a reporting Schedule would not, by itself, create the channel.

### DVLA — contract exists, front door is thin

Contracts Finder notice (DA Languages Ltd, 06207784 — the same company that holds SWL GP interpreting):

> “DVLA currently provides a customer service accessibility provision for deaf customers who use British Sign Language (BSL). … The current Video Relay Service is with DA Languages who provide us with Sign Solutions ‘Interpreters Live!’ service. Since it’s launch in November 2023, the service has been very successful, reflected by the increased take up.”

That is the operational fact. The public `gov.uk/contact-the-dvla` chooser reviewed this pass does not present a BSL button with HMRC- or Companies-House-level clarity. Sibling agency DVSA (tests, not licensing) added InterpretersLive for booking-centre calls in May 2026 after Disability Driving Instructors documented Deaf applicants hitting a wall on the new online booking forms. Do not collapse DVLA and DVSA. Do not write “DfT’s BSL Act duty produced DVLA VRS” — the award notice does not say that.

## 64.4 Does a parent’s Act duty “extend down”?

| Parent on the Schedule | Child in this table | Does the parent’s 5-year BSL plan (Jul 2025) claim the child’s contact channel? |
|---|---|---|
| HM Treasury | HMRC | No. Plan is Treasury digital/social and GCS guidance. |
| AGO | CPS | No. Plan is AGO intranet and Parliament YouTube. |
| MHCLG | Land Registry | MHCLG plan is departmental public-information campaigns. Land Registry not named in the extracts used. |
| DfE | Ofsted | Not checked line-by-line; Ofsted itself publishes no VRS. |
| DHSC | FSA | FSA publishes no VRS. |
| DBT | Companies House | DBT plan URL 404 on this pass. Companies House page does not cite DBT or the Act. |
| DfT | DVLA | Award notice does not cite the Act. |
| MoJ (not a parent of a body in the eight, included as the comparator) | HMCTS, HMPPS, OPG, LAA, CAFCASS | **Yes — unique.** MoJ 5-year plan section 6.6 lists agency BSL translations (HMPPS top-10 family pages by 2030; HMCTS juror BSL review; OPG videos; LAA future video; CAFCASS animations). That is the one ministerial plan located that treats agencies as in-scope for *communications* work. It is still communications films, not a VRS duty, and it is voluntary plan text, not the Act. |

**Finding.** A parent being on the Schedule does not, in published practice, create a BSL contact channel at the agency or NMD that actually answers the phone. Where a channel exists (HMRC, Companies House, DVLA), it is an operational purchase. Where a parent *chooses* to mention agencies (MoJ), it is still about BSL versions of guidance pages. The Act’s own annual reports keep repeating that agencies, NDPBs and ALBs “are not in scope… but are encouraged.” Encouragement is not a cascade.
