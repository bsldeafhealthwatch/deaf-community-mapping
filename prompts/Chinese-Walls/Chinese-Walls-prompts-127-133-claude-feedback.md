## Don't list (print near any workplace / adjustments / City page)

- Do not print that wall-crossing an interpreter is **permitted** or **prohibited** under UK MAR. Art. 18 does not name interpreters. “Capable of reaching” is a reading, not an FCA conclusion (127).
- Do not print that a named bank, Control Room or deal team is unlawful, non-compliant or in breach of the Equality Act or FCA rules. There is no named statute-application, judgment or Final Notice on these facts.
- Do not print exclusion from a wall-crossed meeting or deal team as **automatically** discrimination. s.19 and s.15 carry an objective-justification defence. s.20 is a reasonableness enquiry. The defence is **untested** on these facts (130).
- Do not print “MAR yields to the Equality Act” or “the Equality Act yields to MAR.” No published hierarchy. Do not treat EUWA s.5(A2) (assimilated law subject to incompatible domestic enactments) as a decided answer on these facts — that was a constructed pointer in 127, not a case.
- Do not print SYSC 10.2 as a rule about who may sit *in* a wall-crossed meeting on the informed side. It is a withholding-across-businesses rule. Last update 3 Jul 2016.
- Do not print Art. 18 as “only listed issuers.” Persons acting on the issuer’s behalf or account are in scope. An advising bank’s deal team is typically that person. Still does not decide the interpreter question.
- Do not print NRCPD registration as legally sufficient for MAR. The Code is general confidentiality with a harm / lawful-disclosure carve-out. That carve-out is a *risk* sentence in a live deal room, not a safe harbour (128).
- Do not print a DBS certificate, BPSS, SC or DV clearance as satisfying Art. 18. Different machines. No FCA source treats them as a substitute acknowledgement.
- Do not print the constructed 128 process (list + Art. 18(2) acknowledgement + deal NDA + no notes + off-board) as “industry standard” or “the compliant process.” It is a reading of ordinary third-party kit. Sufficiency unpublished.
- Do not print the Home Office / CCS pack as an FCA rule. Government analogue only. Official Secrets Act ≠ UK MAR.
- Do not print that an AI notetaker in a wall-crossed call **is** market abuse or **is** permitted. No FCA rule names the collision (129).
- Do not print general FCA AI speeches, the 2024 AI Update holding line, the Mills Review or the Supercharged Sandbox as if they decided employee RA inside a wall. Existing-rules-apply is the holding line. Re-check the AI-approach page before reuse — 129 is dated **11 September 2026**.

- Do not print Clifford Chance’s January 2026 boardroom GenAI briefing, Theta Lake vendor PDFs or TakeNote marketing as FCA guidance. Privilege-risk and advice-file products. Wrong object.
- Do not print on-device / no-cloud AI as a documented named-firm RA protocol. Plausible mitigation; unpublished as practice (129 / 130).

- Do not print SEC SECR 6-80, ADA undue hardship, ESMA insider-list Q&As, MiFID II Arts. 16/23, Australian EAF or Canadian NI 31-103 as UK duties (131). SECR 6-80 is the SEC *as employer*.
