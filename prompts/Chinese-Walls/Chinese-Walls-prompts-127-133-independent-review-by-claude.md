## Independent spot-check (before trusting anything else in this file)

Two claims carry the whole batch — if either were wrong, most of the "no published join" headline would collapse.

1. **SYSC 10.2.2 R and its silence on disability, last updated 3 July 2016.** Re-fetched `handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/SYSC/10/2.html` live on 14 September 2026. The page confirms: Chinese Walls provisions let a firm withhold information between business segments where at least one side involves regulated/ancillary/MiFID activity; the "last updated" stamp reads 03/07/2016; the rule text contains no mention of disability, interpreters, or reasonable adjustments anywhere in the body. **Holds verbatim.**

2. **UK MAR Art. 18(1)–(2) wording — "otherwise performing tasks through which they have access," with advisers/accountants/credit rating agencies as the only named examples, and no mention of interpreters.** Re-fetched `legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/596/article/18` live on 14 September 2026. Confirms the "contract of employment, or otherwise performing tasks" wording, the three named examples verbatim (advisers, accountants, credit rating agencies), the Art. 18(2) written-acknowledgement requirement, and no mention of interpreters or communication-support professionals anywhere in the article. **Holds verbatim.**

Both checks pass. The headline claim across all seven files — that Article 18's wording is *capable of reaching* an interpreter but no FCA source confirms that reading, and that SYSC 10.2 is silent on disability — is accurately sourced, not an invented reading dressed up as settled law.

## Don't list (print near any workplace / adjustments / City page)

- Do not print that wall-crossing an interpreter is **permitted** or **prohibited** under UK MAR — Art. 18 does not name interpreters; "capable of reaching" is a reading, independently re-verified as a reading, not an FCA conclusion.
- Do not print that a named bank, Control Room or deal team is unlawful, non-compliant, or in breach of the Equality Act or FCA rules. No named judgment or Final Notice exists on these facts.
- Do not print exclusion from a wall-crossed meeting as **automatically** discrimination — ss.15/19 carry an untested objective-justification defence; s.20 is a reasonableness enquiry.
- Do not print "MAR yields to the Equality Act" or the reverse. No published hierarchy exists. Do not treat EUWA s.5(A2) as a decided answer — it is a constructed pointer, not a case.
- Do not print SYSC 10.2 as a rule about who may sit *in* a wall-crossed meeting on the informed side — it is a withholding-across-businesses rule, independently confirmed silent on disability.
- Do not print NRCPD registration as legally sufficient for MAR purposes — its confidentiality clause has a harm/lawful-disclosure carve-out that is a *risk* sentence in a live deal room, not a safe harbour.
- Do not print a DBS certificate, BPSS, SC, or DV clearance as satisfying Art. 18. Different machines entirely.
- Do not print the constructed 128 seven-step process (list + acknowledgement + NDA + no notes + off-board) as "industry standard." It is a reading of ordinary third-party kit, not a published or observed practice.
- Do not print the Home Office/CCS pack, or Official Secrets Act obligations, as FCA rules. Government analogue only.
- Do not print that an AI notetaker in a wall-crossed call **is** or **is not** market abuse. No FCA rule names the collision, and 129 is explicitly dated and volatile — it should carry its own re-check protocol whenever cited, not be treated as settled after this review.
- Do not print SEC SECR 6-80, ADA undue hardship, ESMA Q&As, MiFID II Arts. 16/23, Australian EAF, or Canadian NI 31-103 as UK duties. All international, all wrong duty-holder or wrong jurisdiction.
- Do not print ABA Formal Opinion 500 as SRA or Law Society policy. International.
- Do not file SigningBanks, LSB, bank customer-VRS pages, HSBC BSL-for-beginners, Lloyds Access/d/Deaf Culture Club, or the Financial News senior-disabled-finance feature as employee-wall-crossing evidence. All are customer access, culture, or progression stories — wrong object, independently confirmed by reading the underlying 132 findings file directly rather than trusting the paraphrase.
- Do not reuse named individuals from earlier batches (Bussey, Clinton, Graham, Rimmer, Clarke, Borys, Elliott, Thomas, Fletcher, Sense ATW0075 employees) in this cell.
- Do not stretch *Locked Out* or *Still Ignored* (health/social care) into a City Control Room context.
- Do not print AIS or GMC guidance as the employee-side answer for an FCA firm — both are patient-side products.
- Do not collapse the Criminal Justice Act 1993 insider-dealing regime into UK MAR Art. 18 — this batch did not run CJA 1993 as a separate cell.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow the two rulebooks and whether anyone has joined them.
