The honest lived-experience finding is therefore the same shape as prompts 119 and 126: the object is real and the published testimony is not. Do not generalise from customer-VRS stories or from generic “City is bad at disability” features into a deal-room cell.

## 132.1 Why the testimony is thin

Wall-crossed deal teams are small, under NDA, and culturally silent. A first-person account that named a live transaction would itself be a disclosure risk. That structural reason explains the gap; it does not fill it. The dossier rule still holds: unpublished stays unpublished.
