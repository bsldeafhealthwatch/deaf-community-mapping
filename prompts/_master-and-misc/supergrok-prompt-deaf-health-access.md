You are a specialist NHS policy and Deaf community health research assistant. I am building a stakeholder matrix and policy briefing for the **Deaf Network Health Group**, a project within the **South West London Integrated Care Board (SWL ICB)** footprint, covering the boroughs of **Croydon, Kingston upon Thames, Merton, Richmond upon Thames, Sutton, and Wandsworth**.

The project focuses on **Deaf and DeafBlind healthcare access** — specifically:
- NHS Accessible Information Standard (AIS) compliance and enforcement
- BSL interpreter provision in primary and secondary care
- DeafBlind specialist services and community identification
- NHS commissioning routes for Deaf and DeafBlind voluntary sector organisations
- Funding landscape for Deaf charities and direct NHS commissioning opportunities

Please search for **current, verified, real-time information** across all five sections below. For each item, include: source name, URL, and date of publication or last update. Flag anything older than 12 months explicitly.

### SECTION 1 — POLICY & LEGISLATION UPDATES (post-June 2025)

Search for the most recent updates on each of the following:

1. **Accessible Information Standard (AIS) — Updated June 2025**
   - What are the specific changes in the revised AIS published 30 June 2025 by NHS England?
   - What is the new sixth "Review" step and what does it require NHS providers to do?
   - What does the requirement for a **senior named AIS lead** in every NHS organisation mean in practice?
   - Is there any progress on making the AIS **mandatory** (currently it is an information standard, not a statutory duty)? What has DHSC said?
   - What is the **NHSE Self-Assessment Framework** published alongside the updated AIS and how should ICBs use it?

2. **Single Patient Record — announced July 2025**
   - What was announced by NHS England about the Single Patient Record in July 2025?
   - How does this affect communication needs flagging for Deaf and DeafBlind patients?
   - What is the implementation timeline?

3. **BSL Act 2022 — enforcement and progress**
   - What is the current status of BSL Act 2022 implementation in England?
   - Has any BSL Advisory Board report been published since the Act came into force?
   - Are there any NHS-specific obligations flowing from the BSL Act?

4. **Data Use and Access Act 2025**
   - What does this Act mean for NHS IT standards and information sharing?
   - How does it affect Deaf patient communication needs records being shared across providers?

5. **NHS England reorganisation / ICB reforms 2025**
   - NHS England is restructuring in 2025–26. What does this mean for ICB commissioning of VCSE / voluntary sector organisations?
   - Has the SWL ICB specifically published any VCSE strategy or Deaf health commissioning update in 2025–26?
   - What is the Cabinet Office VCSE procurement target (two-year direct spending target by April 2026)?

6. **Care Act 2014 — DeafBlind duties**
   - What are local authority statutory duties for deafblind people under the Care Act 2014?
   - Has any statutory guidance been updated since 2014? What does the 2024 DHSC guidance say?
   - How does the AIS interact with Care Act deafblind duties?

### SECTION 2 — POPULATION DATA (Deaf and DeafBlind in the UK and SWL)

Search for the most accurate, most recent estimates for each population group:

**UK National:**
1. Total population of **deaf and hard of hearing** adults in the UK — RNID revised its figure to 18 million in June 2024 using 2021–22 census data. Is there any newer refinement?
2. Total population of **BSL users** in England — the commonly cited figure is 73,000. Is there a more recent estimate?
3. Total population of **DeafBlind** people in the UK — Sense estimates 450,000+ in 2022, projected 610,000 by 2035. Is there more recent data?
4. Population of **deaf children** — NDCS cites 53,225 in 2024 (Consortium for Research into Deaf Education). Has this been updated?
5. **Usher syndrome** (leading cause of acquired deafblindness): ~10,000 in UK (Sense 2010 data). Any recent update?
6. **CHARGE syndrome** (~1 in 10,000 births): Any updated UK incidence data?

**SWL ICB Local:**
7. What is the estimated **Deaf and hard of hearing population** within the SWL ICB six boroughs (Croydon, Kingston, Merton, Richmond, Sutton, Wandsworth)? Use NHS England's hearing loss data tool or ONS local authority data if available.
8. What is the estimated **DeafBlind population** within the SWL ICB footprint? Use Sense prevalence data (per 1,000 population by local authority) if NHS-specific data is unavailable.
9. Are there **local authority Deaf sensory impairment registers** for any of the six SWL boroughs? What do they show?
