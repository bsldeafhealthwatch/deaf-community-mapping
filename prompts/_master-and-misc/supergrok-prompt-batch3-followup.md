### SECTION E2 — Investigating the grassroots-voice gap directly (this is now a finding, not just a search limitation)

6. **Why are there so few Deaf BSL users' own voices on X/social media about NHS access, compared to the volume of charity/organisational content?** Search specifically for: (a) any research, article, or charity statement discussing barriers Deaf BSL users face in posting to text-based social media platforms (X, Facebook) — e.g. whether BSL is a different grammatical structure from written English and this creates a literacy/expression barrier for BSL-dominant users when writing in English text; (b) whether Deaf-specific platforms or channels exist where this kind of complaint DOES appear (e.g. BSL vlogs on YouTube/TikTok, Deaf-specific Facebook groups, SignVideo-style platforms) that a standard X search would miss entirely; (c) any academic or charity commentary on "digital exclusion" specifically affecting BSL-first Deaf people's ability to participate in public online advocacy compared to hearing disabled people or hard-of-hearing (non-signing) people.

7. **Search YouTube, TikTok, and Facebook (not just X) for Deaf BSL vloggers or Deaf-led Facebook groups discussing NHS or healthcare access in England, especially London, in the last 12 months.** This project's prior searches have been X-only; test whether the grassroots voice actually exists but on a different platform. Name specific channels/groups/creators found, with post dates and links, even if not SWL-specific.

8. **Search for any project, charity initiative, or academic study specifically about collecting Deaf BSL users' healthcare experiences in BSL video format** (rather than written English surveys) — this project has already found that Healthwatch SWL's own written patient survey received zero responses, while its BSL video survey option existed. Is there a pattern here — do BSL video-based feedback mechanisms get meaningfully more response than written English ones for this population? Any charity or NHS body that has published a comparison or reflected on this in an evaluation?

### SECTION E3 — New ground: replicability and further PSR/Section 256 examples

9. **Is SignHealth's PSR "Most Suitable Provider" justification (specialist BSL clinical skills, continuity of care) replicable by other Deaf-sector organisations for other services** — search for any other example, anywhere in England, of an ICB using PSR Most Suitable Provider or the urgent-circumstances provision to award a contract to RNID, BDA, Action Deafness, Deafblind UK, or RAD for any health-adjacent service (not just talking therapy — could be interpreting, wellbeing support, health navigation, anything).

10. **Has any Integrated Care Board published guidance, a strategy document, or a board decision specifically encouraging or setting out a pathway for commissioning Deaf-led or BSL-specific VCSE provision** (distinct from the general PSR/VCSE guidance already found)? This is different from asking whether an award has happened — this asks whether any ICB has a stated intention or policy to do this more.
