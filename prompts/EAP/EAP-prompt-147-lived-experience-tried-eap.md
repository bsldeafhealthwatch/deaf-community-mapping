# Prompt 147 — Lived experience of people who *tried* an EAP

**Master index prompt 147.** Lived-experience file in the Employee Assistance Programme batch (prompts 141–149). Compiled 11 September 2026. Dossier rule: stay unbiased; follow money and fulfilment. An unsourced AI claim is the lowest tier. Gaps are logged as gaps.

**What this prompt is for.** First-person from a Deaf, BSL-using, DeafBlind or neurodivergent *employee*, or from an employee calling about a Deaf/disabled family member, who **used or tried** an EAP and found it inaccessible or not fit for purpose. Plus any named campaign, consultation response, union submission or policy ask *about EAP accessibility*. People who never called belong in **149**. Do not reuse Bussey, Clinton, Graham, Rimmer, Clarke, Borys, Murphy, or the OH / Chinese-Walls / AtW named cases as if they were EAP accounts.

## 147.1 What a “tried it” account would have to contain

To move a cell off “no reliable figure found” the source needs: a person (named or stably anonymised), an EAP as the service used, a disability/access fact, and an outcome (could not get through; voice-only; turned away; generic session; used SignHealth instead). SignHealth’s marketing sentence has the conclusion without the person. ReechUs has the outcome without the person or the provider. Royal Mail has the alternative product without the failed call. That is why the testimony cell stays empty.

## 147.2 Case for change that *can* be printed

The printable case is institutional, not testimonial:

1. A Deaf-led charity brought a BSL EAP to market in August 2025 and stated that traditional EAPs are not designed for BSL users.
2. At least one large employer (Royal Mail) bought it.
3. Spectrum.Life publishes a BSL-counsellor-after-chat path and an ND-matching request — which implies the default path is not that path.
4. No union or national Deaf/ND charity was found campaigning on this object.

That is a supply-side case. It is not a documented user journey. 149 asks whether the journey is missing because people never start it.
