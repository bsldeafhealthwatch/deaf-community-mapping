# Prompt 145 — Does any UK EAP document a coordinated response when two access needs arrive together?

**Master index prompt 145.** Compounding-needs file in the Employee Assistance Programme batch (prompts 141–149). Compiled 11 September 2026. Dossier rule: stay unbiased; follow money and fulfilment; do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. An unsourced AI claim is the lowest tier. Gaps are logged as gaps, not filled guesses.

**What this prompt is for.** Test whether a published UK EAP describes a *coordinated* response when the caller has more than one access requirement at once — for example a BSL-using employee who is also neurodivergent, or an employee who needs both a BSL route *and* support about a DeafBlind family member. That is not “the person has stress and a bereavement” (stacked life issues). It is two adjustments that have to be true in the same hour. This prompt tests the premise “not fit for purpose for multiple issues.” It does not assume the premise is true. Do not print that verdict from this file alone.

**Index check.** Wrapper: `prompts/EAP/EAP-prompts-141-149.md`. 142 = one channel (BSL). 143 = one format (ND session). 144 = one third-party subject (family member’s disability). 85–91 already logged the absence of a combined Deaf+ND *diagnostic* pathway. This file is whether an EAP joins two of those needs in one case.

## Headline

**No published UK EAP model was found that coordinates two access requirements in one case.**

What the industry *does* publish as “complexity” is a different object:

- EAPA’s 2024 *Securing the future of the EAP* report and Vivup’s Karl Bennett quote describe rising **clinical intensity** and **stacked life problems** (stress + bullying + bereavement + cost of living + NHS overflow). That is more issues in the same person, still delivered through one telephone / video / F2F door.
- The 2014 EAPA *Counsellors’ Guide* tells affiliate counsellors that some clients “present with complex issues” and should be referred back to the GP, specialist services or crisis lines when short-term work will not hold. That is an **exit** from the EAP, not a protocol for holding BSL *and* an adapted ND format in the same session.

SignHealth’s BSL EAP is a single-axis product: Deaf / BSL-fluent therapy, with lip-reading or SSE as alternative communication preferences. It does not publish a BSL+autism or BSL+family-DeafBlind coordination rule. A January 2026 Signs of Hope (Caritas Westminster) essay by a Deaf therapist states the *ask* — Deaf people also have “races, cultures, sexualities, faiths, and neurodiversities” and should not be grateful for anyone who can sign hello — as something the system does not currently offer as a menu. That is adjacent BSL-therapy commentary, not an EAP specification.

DisabilityPlus tells clients their employer “could fund” specialist cerebral-palsy counselling *through* the EAP, with the employee asking HR to refer. That is a bolt-on specialist reached by breaking self-referral (141), not a multi-need protocol inside the helpline.

BDF’s Great Big Workplace Adjustments Survey 2023 records whether disabled employees *use* an EAP as a wellbeing metric. It does not describe an EAP that coordinates two adjustments at once.

No named complaint or case was found in which an EAP is recorded as failing *because* two access needs arrived together. Locked Out’s intersectional chapter is health and social care, not EAP.

The absence of a coordinated-multi-need EAP model is the finding. Do not convert that absence into “not fit for purpose.”

**Hard-don't.** Do not print the premise as a verdict. Do not file EAPA “complexity of need” as access-coordination. Do not file 85–91’s missing Deaf+ND diagnostic pathway as an EAP finding.

## 145.1 Two different “complexities”

Print the distinction or the cell collapses.

| EAPA / market “complexity” | This prompt’s object |
|---|---|
| More clinical severity; more life issues in one person; NHS overflow | Two *access adjustments* that must both be true in the same hour |
| Response: longer work, refer out, case-manage risk | Response that would be needed: BSL *and* agenda/summary; or BSL *and* family-DeafBlind content, held together |
| Documented in the 2024 report | Not documented as an EAP model this pass |

A caller who is Deaf *and* autistic, or who needs BSL *and* is calling about a DeafBlind partner, would on the published specs meet either a generic helpline (142/143/144) or a single-axis specialist (SignHealth). Nothing found joins them.

## 145.2 What is unpublished or contested

Unpublished:

- Any EAP service specification, case-manager SOP or affiliate instruction that says “if the caller needs BSL and an adapted format, do both.”
- Any named complaint whose pleaded facts are the *join* of two access needs.
- Any utilisation split (how many EAP users present with two disability-related access needs).

Contested / do not inflate:

- Signs of Hope “menu not a ration” is a BSL-therapy supply argument. True as commentary; not an EAP audit.
- DisabilityPlus “we work with many EAP companies” is a funding claim, not a coordination protocol.

The premise “not fit for purpose for multiple issues” remains an untested hypothesis after this pass. 147 (lived experience of people who tried) and 149 (people who never called) are the next places it could surface as testimony. It does not surface here as a documented model or a documented failure.
