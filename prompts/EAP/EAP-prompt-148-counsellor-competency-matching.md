# Prompt 148 — Do EAP counsellors have documented training or case-matching for Deaf, BSL, DeafBlind or neurodivergent profiles?

**Master index prompt 148.** Competency / matching file in the Employee Assistance Programme batch (prompts 141–149). Compiled 11 September 2026. Dossier rule: stay unbiased; follow money and fulfilment. Do not print that a named provider or counsellor is unqualified or negligent without a BACP, UKCP, tribunal or equivalent finding.

**What this prompt is for.** Whether the *person answering* has documented training or is matched to the caller’s profile. Not 142 (channel), not 143 (session format), not 145 (two adjustments held together). Test matching and referral-out as published practice, not an assumed deficit.

**Index check.** Wrapper: `prompts/EAP/EAP-prompts-141-149.md`. SignHealth’s whole product is a specialist roster — in scope as matching-by-buying-a-different-EAP, not as proof that Health Assured matches.

---

## Headline

**No mandatory EAP-counsellor module on Deaf culture, BSL-mediated therapy, neurodivergent-affirming practice or compounding disability profiles was found.** Assignment on the mainstream helpline is generic unless the caller (or the contract) asks.

What exists, kept apart:

- **Profession-wide, not EAP-specific.** BACP *GPaCP 007 Working with disability* (November 2025) is an open-access good-practice note that “assumes limited prior knowledge” and points to further CPD. It is not a competence framework and is not written into EAPA UK Standards. SCoPEd (January 2022) includes an ability to work with a third party in the room, “e.g. as translator, interpreter, **signer**, carer.” That is interpreter-in-the-room competence, not BSL-fluent therapy. BACP’s *Workplace counselling competence framework* (February 2021) tells workplace counsellors to know the organisation’s disability support services and to refer out concerns “that lie outside the remit of counselling (e.g. disabilities…).” Limits-of-capability language, not a Deaf/ND syllabus.
- **Optional CPD market.** SQA HN unit *Counselling: Deaf People* (H0HL 35, SCQF 8) exists in Scotland as a specialist unit. Private certificates (Basingstoke *Working with Neurodivergent Clients* 2026; Grove inclusivity module; autism-informed CPD) are for-sale extras. None is an EAP induction requirement.
- **Published matching, on request or by buying a different product.** Spectrum.Life staff FAQs: autistic employee may request a counsellor with neurodivergent experience “when possible”; hard-of-hearing / cannot use the phone → Live Chat then “video counselling with a counsellor who knows British Sign Language.” AXA EAP brochure (Spectrum.Life delivery) lists “neurodiversity specialists” among a 100+ clinician team — insurance/EAP brochure language, not a published roster with headcount. SignHealth’s BSL EAP *is* the roster: Deaf or BSL-fluent therapists. Spectrum.Life’s advertised *Neurodiversity Assessment Service* jobs are a separate diagnostic product, not the 24/7 advice-line.
- **Referral-out is the documented safety valve.** EAPA *Counsellors’ Guide* (2014): if the presentation will not fit short-term work, refer back to the GP or specialist services via the case manager. That is exit, not match. DisabilityPlus (145) is specialist counselling funded *through* the EAP after an HR referral.

**Mismatch testimony.** No BACP, UKCP, NCS or tribunal finding against a named EAP counsellor on these facts. ReechUs 20 May 2025 remains the only UK-toned “wrong specialist / generic CBT” cluster — unnamed, vendor, already flagged.

Do not collapse “we have neurodiversity specialists” (brochure) or “you can request” (FAQ) into “the helpline is matched by default.”

**Hard-don't.** No negligence claim without a named professional finding. Do not file BACP GPaCP 007 as a mandatory EAP module.

## 148.1 Three matching models, not one

| Model | Who does it | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Generic affiliate + refer out | Mainstream EAPA model | Short-term generalist; exit if the profile will not fit |
| On-request match | Spectrum.Life FAQ; HA “subject to request” | Caller or employer must ask; “when possible” |
| Buy a specialist EAP | SignHealth BSL EAP | The roster *is* the specialism; HR/employer refers |

None of those is “the 24/7 voice line is staffed by BSL-fluent, ND-affirming, compounding-need clinicians as standard.”

## 148.2 What is unpublished or contested

Unpublished:

- Any EAP affiliate-induction syllabus that names Deaf culture or ND-affirming practice as a required hour.
- A public roster (n=) of BSL-knowing or ND-specialist EAP counsellors at Health Assured, Vivup, Bupa or Validium.
- A BACP professional-conduct finding on an EAP mismatch.

Contested / do not inflate:

- “Neurodiversity specialists” on an AXA brochure page. True as a sentence; unpublished as a helpline headcount.
- “When possible” matching. The phrase is the finding that matching is contingent.
