## Verdict for any public page

**Curate first. Draft almost nothing.**

The one sentence a page is allowed to lead with, if any:

> A workplace EAP is usually a voice-first, confidential helpline your employer has bought. It is designed so HR does not know you called. That design is also why booking a BSL interpreter for the same call often has nowhere to go.

What already exists and should be linked, not rewritten:

| Need | Point at |
|---|---|
| Employer still owes s.20 on a voluntary benefit | Equality Act s.39(2)(b) + s.39(5). ACAS RA page (interpreter as an example aid) and ACAS EAP-confidentiality page are **two pages**. Do not join them as if ACAS already did. |
| Mainstream EAP access as advertised | That employer’s own intranet / provider pack. Catalogue extras (Relay UK, Live Chat, “BSL counsellor subject to request”) are buyer-PDF claims, not a staff-facing VRS queue. |
| BSL-fluent therapy as a *different product* | SignHealth BSL EAP — employer refers; 90-minute assessment then a 10-session course; 2025/26 fees on the offer PDF. Not a 24/7 self-referral helpline. |
| NHS BSL talking therapy (wrong object, useful door) | SignHealth NHS Talking Therapies contracts already in the health trackers. Do not caption them as the workplace EAP. |
| AtW Mental Health Support Service (wrong object) | Able Futures / Maximus AtW MHSS. Nine-month workplace mental-health coaching. Not an EAP. |
| Family / carer stress (generic) | Carers UK; the employer’s EAP family line if it has one. EIM 21845: family/dependant EAP cover is **not** tax-exempt. |
| Stress-claim legal frame | *Hatton v Sutherland* [2002] IRLR 263 — confidential advice + counselling makes a breach finding “unlikely,” not impossible. ACAS duty-of-care page. |

Thin original drafts worth doing *later*, after a human signs this file off:

1. A **one-screen product chooser** — mainstream EAP / SignHealth BSL EAP / NHS Deaf talking therapy / AtW MHSS. Four different payers, four different doors. The collision in 141 is why the chooser exists.
2. A **four-line staff card** for a known BSL-using workforce: “If you cannot use the voice line, here is the text/chat path the contract actually names, and here is who can book SignHealth without you explaining the problem to your manager.” Only after a named employer’s pack is opened.
3. A **public-body FOI question** (see ranked list below). Private EAP providers are not FOIA bodies.

Do not write “EAPs are inaccessible to Deaf staff” as a national rate. Do not write “employers buy EAPs to avoid tribunals.”

## Don't list (print near any EAP / workplace-wellbeing page)

- Do not print that a named EAP provider is unlawful, non-compliant, or in breach of the Equality Act.
- Do not print that contracting the EAP **discharges** s.20. 121 already holds the opposite for operational desks; 141 found no source that says the EAP contract is the exception.
- Do not print that providers are “contractually barred” from naming users as if that were a statute. EAPA s.7 is a membership standard plus contract plus GDPR plus BACP — not a prohibition Act.
- Do not treat the 12 July 2024 BBC File on 4 Health Assured report as an ICO or court finding, and do not file it as a Deaf/BSL case.
- Do not caption SignHealth’s BSL EAP as a self-referral 24/7 helpline. The published path is **employer referral**.
- Do not treat built-in English captions, Relay UK, or Live Chat as discharging a request for a BSL-fluent therapist.
- Do not file Spectrum.Life “request a neurodivergent-experienced counsellor when possible” as a format rule. That is matching (148), not agenda / written summary / text-not-call (143).
- Do not file Spectrum.Life / AXA / Bupa insurer **neurodiversity assessment** products as EAP counselling.
- Do not file EAPA 2024 “complexity” (stacked life issues + NHS overflow + refer-out) as a two-access-need protocol (145).
- Do not paste ADA, EEOC, FCC VRS, Australian EAF, EAA 24/7 VRS, or Dutch UWV Tolkcontact hours into a UK cell (146).
- Do not paste Canada’s Health Canada TTY number into a UK staff leaflet.
- Do not generalise ReechUs 20 May 2025 “five managers” into a survey.
- Do not reuse Bussey, Clinton, Graham, Rimmer, Clarke, Borys, Murphy, or the OH / Chinese-Walls / AtW named cases as EAP accounts.
- Do not print a Deaf/BSL EAP utilisation rate. None was found. The missing split is the finding (149).
- Do not pick 3–5% *or* 12% as “the” official UK utilisation figure without naming the source. EAPA 2024’s own 2022 sentence is “topped 12%”; 3–5% is vendor/trade-press.
- Do not print “employers buy EAPs to limit liability” as a motive. Print *Hatton*’s words, or print CIPD’s 41% / public-sector 70% offer-rate, or print nothing.
- Do not print BDF 2023 “less than half of disabled employees said the EAP was accessible and inclusive” as a Deaf figure. All-disability sample.
- Do not print family EAP cover as a tax-free benefit. EAPA / HMRC March 2023: EIM 21845.
- Do not print “HSE requires a counsellor who signs.”
- Do not collapse this page into 121–126, 92–96, 97–102, 144 into 150–157, or 145 into 85–91.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment — who pays per session, who has to know you called, whether the published door is voice.

## What to do with this finding

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| Someone asks for “the number” of Deaf staff using EAPs | There isn’t one. Say so. Point at 149. Whole-workforce EAPA 2022 usage topped 12%; no disability cut. |
| Someone wants a letter / template to their employer | Curate: ask HR for the **written** access route in the current EAP contract (text, chat, video, BSL-knowing counsellor, SignHealth add-on) and whether using that route requires a management referral. Do not write “your EAP is unlawful.” |
| Someone wants a letter to SignHealth / HA / Spectrum.Life | Not FOIA. A right-of-reply email is fine. Do not publish a compliance score. |
| Someone wants a new domain page | No. One stub paragraph under Welfare & Employment, plus the chooser above if a human signs it off. |
| Someone wants to cite Hatton as “EAPs win stress claims” | Quote the sentence. Add the commentary line: not an absolute defence. Separate from s.20. |
| Someone wants to treat Royal Mail as the national model | One named purchaser of SignHealth’s product. Not a census. |

## What actually closes the gap (ranked)

1. **FOI to public employers that already sit in the companion log** — ICB / NHS trust / local authority / government department:  
   - “Name the current EAP provider and contract end date.”  
   - “Does the live staff-facing service offer a non-voice route (Relay UK / Live Chat / email / video)? Paste the staff instruction, not the buyer brochure.”  
   - “Is a BSL-fluent or SignHealth pathway in the contract, or only ‘subject to request’?”  
   Do **not** lead with “utilisation by disability.” They will not hold it (149). Use an existing T-template; do not stand up a new tracker.
2. **Link the live resources** — SignHealth BSL EAP page; that employer’s own EAP pack; ACAS mental-health + RA pages as two links; *Hatton* only if the page is talking about stress claims, not s.20.
3. **A dated stub sentence** on the existing Welfare & Employment briefing: the 141 bind in one paragraph, SignHealth as the specialist add-on, no uptake split.
4. **A new draft** — only the chooser card at verdict §1, after a human signs this file. No second wellbeing microsite.

Nice-to-have that does **not** need a new prompt number:

- Re-open EAPA Standards after any 2026 replacement; BACP Ethical Framework on **3 Nov 2026**.
- If a public employer FOI returns a staff-facing BSL instruction that is *not* a buyer PDF, patch 142 with that URL.
- If anyone later finds a named first-person who tried or refused the EAP, that is a 147/149 patch, not prompt 164.

## Cross-batch rules

- Distinct from **121–126** because confidentiality and self-referral are the *design*, and because the family-member caller and the counsellor-competency question do not arise on an IT desk.
- Distinct from **92–96** because OH reports to the employer; the EAP is sold as the opposite.
- Distinct from **97–102** because the usual interpreter-booking ticket is the thing the EAP is built to bypass.
- Distinct from **127–133** because the third party here would hear a therapeutic disclosure, not a deal.
- Distinct from **143 vs 148** — format of the hour vs who sits in the chair. Keep them split.
- Distinct from **144 vs 150–157** — EAP family *content* vs statutory leave / Art. 8 / household stacking.
- Distinct from **145 vs 85–91** — two adjustments in one EAP hour vs a combined diagnostic pathway.
- Shared facts that must not be reprinted as if this batch discovered them: AtW cap £69,260 and 3,210 BSL-interpreter customers (52–54 / 20–26); no national non-emergency VRS in England (78–80); civil servants excluded from AtW from the 13 Nov 2025 staff-guide edit (121–126 memory) — relevant *context* for why a civil-service EAP door matters, not a 141–149 finding.
- International material in 146 stays international.
- Next free number is **164** (Household-Leave 150–157 and TUPE 158–163 already used 150–163). Do not recycle 150.
