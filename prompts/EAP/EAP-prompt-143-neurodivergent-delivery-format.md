# Prompt 143 — Do UK EAPs publish an adapted session format for neurodivergent employees?

**Master index prompt 143.** Delivery-format file in the Employee Assistance Programme batch (prompts 141–149). Compiled 11 September 2026. Dossier rule: stay unbiased; follow money and fulfilment; do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. An unsourced AI claim is the lowest tier. Gaps are logged as gaps, not filled guesses.

**What this prompt is for.** Test whether any named UK EAP *publishes a session format* adapted for neurodivergent users — structured / agenda-led sessions, written follow-up summaries, a documented choice of text over a live call — as distinct from “we support all employees.” Record any UK survey, charity report or first-person that describes unstructured phone counselling as accessible or not. This is **not** 85–91 (diagnostic pathways) and **not** 148 (whether the counsellor has a named competency). Format only.

**Index check.** Wrapper: `prompts/EAP/EAP-prompts-141-149.md`. Gate is 141 (confidentiality). 142 is the BSL *channel*. Insurance neurodiversity *assessment* products (Bupa, Aviva, Spectrum.Life insurer pathway) stay out of the EAP-format cell.

## Headline

No named UK EAP provider was found this pass that publishes an adapted **session format** for neurodivergent service users — agenda in advance, written session summary, shorter or broken sessions, sensory-adjusted delivery, or a documented right to do the work in text instead of a live call — as a standard product feature. What exists is:

- **Channel choice in English** already logged in 142 (live chat, email, SMS, WhatsApp, video). That is a door, not a structured session.
- **Counsellor matching on request**, which is prompt 148. Closest published sentence: Spectrum.Life’s Queen’s University Belfast FAQ (PDF dated 19 December 2025) — “I am autistic, when and how do I ask for a counsellor with specific expertise… Yes, this can be requested and when possible we will arrange a counsellor who has experience in working with neurodivergent people.” That answers *who* is on the call, not *how the call is run*.
- **Insurance / clinical assessment products** sold next to EAPs (Bupa neurodiversity benefit; Aviva neurodevelopmental pathway; Spectrum.Life insurer neurodiversity services). Those are diagnosis-and-CBT pathways, not EAP helpline format.

EAPA UK Standards (December 2022 / January 2023) require a Diversity & Inclusion policy and “sufficient… diversity” of staffing. They do not specify a neurodivergent session format. ACAS neurodiversity pages tell *employers* to use written records, agendas and extra time in capability / grievance meetings. They do not tell EAP providers how to run a counselling hour.

NAS, ADHD UK and the British Dyslexia Association were not found to publish an EAP-format survey or a first-person EAP-phone account this pass. NAS, as an employer, buys Health Assured for its own staff — a generic 24/7 EAP, with no published format rider.

The only UK-toned first-person-adjacent material found is a 20 May 2025 vendor blog (ReechUs) reporting that “five different managers” said employees who disclosed autism or ADHD were turned away because the EAP “didn’t have the right specialist” or offered generic CBT when “what they need is strategy, structure and forward motion.” Unnamed providers, unnamed employees, vendor promoting an alternative. **Thin / anecdotal.** Do not generalise it. The “specialist” limb is 148; the “structure” limb is this prompt, and it remains unsourced as a measured finding.

NHS talking-therapy and community-counselling pages *do* publish format adjustments (TALKWORKS: shorter or extended sessions, easy-read / audio handouts, lighting, venue video; Spectra London: shorter sessions, breaks, reminders, audio-only, stim-friendly). That contrast is useful context: the format language exists in adjacent UK therapy settings and has not been found on EAP product pages. It is not an EAP finding.

International only, not a UK cell: US “EAP Expert” article listing written summaries and flexible structures; Australian NEAP helpline.

**Hard-don't.** Do not print that a named EAP is “not fit for purpose” for neurodivergent staff. Do not file *Duncan v Fujitsu* (written communication / agendas at work) as an EAP-format judgment. Do not file Bupa/Aviva assessment products as EAP session format.

## 143.1 What was being tested

The prompt’s examples (agenda-led sessions, written follow-ups, text instead of a live call) are format. Matching a “neurodivergent-experienced counsellor” is personnel. Assessment-and-CBT add-ons sold by the same corporate groups are a third product. Mixing the three would manufacture a “yes they adapt” cell from a “we have specialists / we do assessments / we have live chat” stack. They were kept apart.

## 143.2 What is unpublished or contested

Unpublished:

- Any UK EAP service specification that requires an agenda, a written summary, or a text-session option for neurodivergent users.
- Any NAS / ADHD UK / BDA / BDF survey question on EAP *format*.
- Any named first-person of an EAP phone hour that failed (or worked) because of structure, sensory load, or lack of a written recap.

Contested / thin:

- ReechUs “five managers.” Use only as a flagged anecdote that the *structure* ask exists in the market conversation. It does not measure prevalence.
- Whether live chat on an EAP app is in practice used as a format adjustment. No source says so.

Do not run this silence forward as “EAPs are not fit for purpose for neurodivergent employees.” That is the 145 premise, and 148 still has to test whether matching exists.
