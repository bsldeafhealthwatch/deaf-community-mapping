## Prompt text

As a comparative social-policy researcher, using peer-reviewed journals (e.g., Journal of Deaf Studies and Deaf Education, Ageing & Society, Disability & Society), international Deaf health and ageing research bodies (e.g., National Center for Deaf Health Research in the US, European Union of the Deaf), and government or NGO reports from the US, EU member states, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand, identify research and policy approaches to older Deaf and DeafBlind people's care and social inclusion outside the UK. Cover: (1) any country or region with a documented Deaf-specific residential care model (a Deaf retirement community, Deaf-staffed care home, or similar) and evidence of outcomes compared with mainstream placement, (2) research measuring loneliness or wellbeing in Deaf elders internationally and how it compares with UK findings (or the UK's absence of findings), (3) transport, communicator-guide, or befriending schemes for older Deaf/DeafBlind people documented as working well enough to be studied or replicated, (4) any national policy (statutory right, funding stream, or planning requirement) that specifically addresses older Deaf/DeafBlind people's social care or isolation, rather than leaving them inside general elderly-disability policy. For each source, provide title, author/institution, country, publication date, and URL, and note which findings might translate usefully to the UK context, particularly the South West London ICB footprint this project covers.
