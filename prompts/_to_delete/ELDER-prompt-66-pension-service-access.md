## Prompt text

As a UK welfare-policy researcher specializing in disability access and the Accessible Information Standard (AIS), using GOV.UK guidance for the State Pension and Pension Service, DWP's BSL video relay service provision, Pension Credit application guidance, CQC/PHSO findings, and any National Audit Office or Work and Pensions Select Committee reports touching accessible communication for older claimants, identify the specific barriers BSL-using Deaf and DeafBlind pensioners face when claiming or managing the State Pension, Pension Credit, and related retirement benefits (Attendance Allowance, Winter Fuel Payment / Pension Age Winter Heating Payment where relevant). Cover: (1) whether the Pension Service's BSL VRS product (InterpretersLive or equivalent) is actually offered at the point an older Deaf claimant needs it, versus buried in general contact pages, (2) Pension Credit's notoriously low take-up rate and whether any published research or FOI response breaks that figure out by disability or deafness specifically, (3) DWP's record on accessible correspondence (large print, BSL video, easy read) for state pension award letters and annual uprating notices, (4) any CQC, PHSO, or Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman findings naming a Deaf or DeafBlind pensioner failed by DWP/Pension Service communication. For each finding, give the source title, publishing body, date, and direct URL. Flag plainly wherever a body publishes nothing findable — that absence is data, not a failed search.
