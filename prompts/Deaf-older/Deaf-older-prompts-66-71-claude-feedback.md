## Don’t list (print near any later-life page)

- Do not print Community Life Survey **14%** (disabled adults lonely) or **4–5%** (65+) as a Deaf-elder rate.
- Do not print RNID **36%** lonely/isolated as “over a third of older Deaf people.” It is a BSL-user *stigma subset*, not age-banded.
- Do not print the Finnish **36%** as UK.
- Do not print Sense’s **250,000 deafblind over 70** as a measured SWL or BSL figure. Modelled, dual-sensory, not a loneliness rate.
- Do not write “Deaf older people automatically get a Freedom Pass.” Threshold is **70 dBHL both ears**, or SPA. Moderate age-related loss is outside the deafness category.
- Do not write that Taxicard is a Deaf product. Automatic entry is mobility / SSI-blind.
- Do not write that London issues companion Freedom Passes. It does not. Croydon’s Companion Badge is parking anti-theft.
- Do not describe TfL as having live next-stop BSL. It has a YouTube explainer. Luna / c2c / LNER / SWR are *trials*.
- Do not write “AIS applies to the Pension Service.” It does not. Equality Act reasonable adjustment + BSL Act reporting on DWP as a listed ministerial department.
- Do not collapse deafblind specialist-assessor duty (Care Act 6.91–6.96) into the general 6.80 interpreter line for BSL-Deaf adults who are not deafblind.
- Do not repeat *Locked Out* “only 2 residential homes that cater specifically to BSL users” without the named-list contest in prompt 67.
- Do not import Alan Graham, Samantha, Pearl Clinton or Francesca Bussey as Domain 17 case studies. Pointer only.
- Do not present the ICE 2024/25 customer as “DWP refused BSL.” Published failures are address, suspension, extra-support consideration.
- Do not present Manchester “Margret” or Locked Out speakers as named public figures.
- Do not hold the page out as a welfare-rights service, a care-home finder, or legal advice.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment.

### 3. Commissioning — pick one of four models and cost it

Prompt 70 left the menu on the table. A Domain 17 page that lists all four and walks away is a reading list.

| Model | Overseas proof | UK hook | Honest limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Campus** | De Gelderhorst (117 apartments + nursing); Bob Rumball 64 beds; NEHD Danvers | Care Act placement + specialist residential procurement; Longley Road is the local existence proof | Capital + staffing. One national home is also an uprooting cost (prompt 69) |
| **Mobile signing workforce** | Swedish Deaf assistant-nurse team (*BMC Geriatrics* Dec 2025, n=4) | LA home care / direct payments / VCSE contract; possible ICB prevention | Evidence base is four people. Needs Deaf-led provider, not “sensory support” |
| **65+ interpreting fund** | Australia NSLP (not-on-NDIS, aged care + Medicare + bank/funeral) | DHSC or DWP line for people with no social-care package | Different welfare state. Closest UK analogue is “too old for AtW, too language-specific for Age UK” |

Near-term product this site can defend: **do not campaign for a campus in the next quarter.** Point SWL commissioners at (a) Remark-style rooms that already exist, (b) Longley Road as a rated home, (c) the FOI count, (d) the Care Act split. A campus ask without a placement count is theatre.

## Page structure if Claude drafts anything

One internal Domain 17 stub. One optional public “later life” page, same skeleton as the AtW/PIP guides:

1. **What this is / is not.** England social care and DWP pensions. Scotland has Contact Scotland BSL and a BSL Act of its own — do not paste England Care Act copy into a Scotland toggle. Not end-of-life. Not AtW.
2. **Three doors today.** Pension Service VRS. Local-authority adult-social-care front door (name BSL / deafblind). Remark Croydon or the person’s nearest Deaf club.
3. **The Care Act split, in four lines.** Deafblind = specialist assessor. BSL-Deaf and not deafblind = general interpreter duty. *Locked Out* has asked to close that. That is a fact about guidance, not a finding of illegality.
4. **Travel, in four lines.** 70 dBHL or SPA. No London companion pass. Taxicard is mobility / severe sight.
5. **What we cannot count.** Loneliness rate, SWL BSL-home census, Freedom Pass-by-deafness stock. Then the FOI list in one sentence.
6. **Disclaimer.** Not legal advice, not a placement service, not Age UK.
