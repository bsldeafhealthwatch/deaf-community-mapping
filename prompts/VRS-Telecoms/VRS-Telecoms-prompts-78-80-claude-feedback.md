## Verdict for any public page

**Curate first. Draft almost nothing.**

The one sentence the page is allowed to lead with:

> In England and Wales a Deaf BSL user cannot pick up a phone and dial any UK number the way a hearing person can. The commercial product is a directory of organisations that have bought a listing. 999 BSL and (for Scotland-resident users) Contact Scotland BSL are the two public exceptions.

What already exists and should be linked, not rewritten:

| Need | Point at |
|---|---|
| Emergency in BSL | 999 BSL app / `999bsl.co.uk`. Free, 24/7, zero-rated data *should* apply. Download before you need it. |
| Everyday any-number VRS if you live in Scotland | Contact Scotland BSL — Sign Solutions from 1 Dec 2025. `contactscotland-bsl.org` |
| Call a bank / utility / named public body that has bought VRS | That body’s own page, then the supplier directory it names (SignVideo SignDirectory, InterpretersLive, Convo Community Directory). Ofcom’s consumer page already does this. |
| Talk to HMRC in BSL | Prompt 55 hosts — Extra Support + Debt Management SignVideo. Do not invent a third HMRC VRS. |
| Workplace calling | Access to Work (prompts 52–54) + the employer’s own VRS if it has one. Do not tell the reader AtW VRS “travels with you” to a new job. |
| Text any number today | Relay UK (18001 + number, or the app). This is the mandated *text* product. Do not caption it as BSL. |

Thin original drafts worth doing *later*, after a human signs this file off:

1. A **one-screen “there is no UK any-number BSL phone”** box. That thinness *is* the finding (prompt 78).
2. A **chooser**: emergency → 999 BSL. Scotland resident → Contact Scotland. Named organisation → check that directory. Anyone else → Relay UK (text) or an AtW/personal minute bundle if you have one.
3. A **four-line “please take this VRS call — the interpreter is not a third party”** script for banks and utilities. Label it as this site’s wording. Do not write “refusing is illegal.”
4. A **travel strip** (see airline addendum below): which UK airlines and which UK airports currently sell a directory listing, with hours. Re-check every listing before publish.

Do not write a US TRS explainer as if it were a UK product. Do not write “Ofcom banned everyday VRS.” Ofcom declined to *mandate* it in 2019.

## Don’t list (print near any VRS / telecoms page)

- Do not write “UK VRS works like a phone number.”
- Do not write “refusing a VRS call is illegal.” No UK judgment found. EHRC 2026 services Code *names* video interpreting as an example adjustment. Ireland 2022 (Kalinova v Permanent TSB) is not UK law. The Claire / Barclays fraud-alert story is one advocacy anecdote.
- Do not print a national count of VRS-contracted organisations. “Over 150” is SignVideo Play Store copy, not a dated census.
- Do not print PIP as an official VRS tariff. That is Convo’s help-page wording, not a DWP circular.
- Do not write “Access to Work VRS is portable.” GOV.UK employer factsheet: support-worker awards do not automatically transfer on a job change.
- Do not reprint the £69,260 cap or the 109-day wait as if they originated in 78–80. Point at prompt 52.
- Do not collapse Relay UK (mandated text, any number) with commercial VRS (directory or user-paid).
- Do not treat Contact Scotland as available to an England-resident user as of right.
- Do not treat 10 Mbit/s USO as a certified VRS bitrate.
- Do not claim an MNO sells universal BSL calling.
- Do not invent a reachable-set ratio (150 ÷ millions of UK numbers).
- Do not print Australia or New Zealand as 24/7 video relay.
- Do not print the European Accessibility Act as a VRS-fund statute. It is RTT / total conversation / 112.
- Do not print the US model as “free” without the carrier contribution factor and the FY 2025–26 VRS line of about **$661 million**.
- Do not print a US fraud-dollar total that was not opened from an FCC enforcement order on this pass.
- Do not write “the UK has considered and rejected the US model.” Sourced trail: emergency mandated; everyday left to the market plus Scotland.
- Do not hold the page out as legal advice, an Ofcom complaint service, or a VRS retailer.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment. Minutes, hours and who pays are the fulfilment facts.

## Addendum — airlines and airports (domestic and international)

Prompt 79 asked what exists for an unplanned call “while away from a pre-arranged VRS contract (contacting a GP, reporting to police, a delayed-train or taxi/delivery query).” Airlines and airports are the same travel row. They were not in the original 78–80 question list. This addendum closes that hole.

**The model is still prompt 78.** No UK airline gives the passenger a portable number that can reach *any* airline. Each carrier that offers BSL VRS has **bought a listing** with Convo / SignLive or SignVideo. Hours are the carrier’s contact-centre hours, not 24/7, except where a press release says otherwise. Regulation (EC) 1107/2006 (retained) requires assistance and “communication of essential information concerning a flight in accessible formats.” It does **not** require a VRS contract. CAA CAP2241 interprets 1107; it is not a VRS mandate.

Re-open every row before a public travel strip. SignLive has been rebranding to Convo through 2025–26; BA’s own pages still mix the two names.

## What actually closes the gap

Ranked by what the instrument can really do. Sell each layer as itself.

### 1. Use the two public services that already exist

| Lever | Who | What it can do | What it cannot do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 999 BSL (GC C5.11–C5.12) | Ofcom + SLI + BT wholesale + every UK CP | Emergency calling in BSL, 24/7, free, no pre-registration | A GP, a 101 that is not in a directory, an airline, a landlord |
| Contact Scotland BSL | Scottish Government + Sign Solutions (from 1 Dec 2025), ~£3m published award | Scotland-resident user, UK-number reach, 24/7, inbound if registered | England/Wales entitlement. Confirm work/international on the 2025 spec before reprinting old limits |
| Relay UK (text) | BT NGTR + every UK CP | Any UK number, today, in text | BSL |

### 2. Count the commercial directory — do not moralise it

The UKCoD directory + SignVideo SignDirectory + Convo Community Directory *are* the map of who has paid. A public page that lists “banks that have VRS” should link those directories and date the scrape. It should not score organisations “non-compliant with the Act.” DAGM prompt 42 already forbids that sentence.

Airline / airport listings belong on the same scrape tab, with hours.

### 3. Law — name the stack, do not invent a VRS duty

| Lever | Who | What it can do | What it cannot do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equality Act s.20 / s.29 + EHRC 2026 services Code (“video interpreting services”) | Service provider | Anticipatory reasonable-adjustment hook; a complaint and a four-line script | A decided UK case that hanging up a VRS call is a breach |
| Ofcom GC C5.8–C5.12 | Communications providers | Text relay + emergency video relay | Everyday VRS |
| BSL Act 2022 | Ministerial departments’ communications | Annual report on promotion of BSL. HMRC-style gap sits with Treasury, not Ofcom (prompt 61 discipline) | A telecoms VRS fund. Ofcom is not a ministerial department in the Act’s reporting sense |
| Regulation 1107/2006 (retained) + CAA CAP2241 | Airlines and UK airports | Assistance + essential flight information in accessible formats | A VRS contract |
| *Locked Out* priority (3), 27 Nov 2025 | BSL Advisory Board recommendation | Political hook for a national E&W VRS | Not government policy. Not a specification. |
| US 47 USC § 225 / Canada NCF / Australia NRS levy | Foreign | Feature map for prompt 80 | Not a UK lever. Do not write “we should have the FCC” as a legal conclusion on an England page |

### 4. Workplace calling — point at 52–54, add one sentence

AtW will pay for VRS minutes. The award is capped, slow, and **not self-transferring** when the employer changes. RNID 2026 records advisers pushing VRS as a cheaper substitute and cutting VRS minutes at renewal. That sentence belongs on the AtW page, with a cross-link here, not as a new VRS treatise.

### 5. FOI / right of reply — fold into the existing companion log

Do **not** build a VRS-only letter wave. Add at most three frozen questions to bodies already in cycle 0:

| Body | Ask |
|---|---|
| Named SWL / SEL trusts + ICB already on T1/T2 | Last FY: did you hold a VRS or VRI contract? Supplier name. Minutes used. Unit rate if held. Found / not-found / refused. |
| Six SWL councils | Same one-liner for public-facing phone lines (housing, social care, council tax). |
| NHSE / SignHealth (right of reply, not FOI if they are not a FOI body in the usual way — NHSE is) | Is there a live national health VRS successor to BSL Health Access (closed Mar 2021)? Yes/no + URL. *Locked Out* says a recommendation was accepted and unimplemented. |
| Contact Scotland / Scottish Government | Confirm 2025 specification: work-related calls, international calls, England-resident eligibility. Reply overrides the old project note. |

Do not FOI “how many BSL users in Wandsworth can make a VRS call from home.” They will not know.

### 6. What not to build

No second FOI platform. No RAG “VRS compliance” dashboard. No AI-filled SignDirectory scrape presented as an official statistic. No US-style 10-digit numbering plan drawn on a napkin. Static HTML table + dated CSV of *who has a listing this month* is enough, and only if a human wants it.
