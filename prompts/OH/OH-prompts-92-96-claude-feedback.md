## Don’t list (print near any work / OH / adjustments page)

- Do not print that Occupational Health itself is under the Equality Act s.20 duty. The duty-holder is the **employer**. OH advice can put the employer on notice. It is not a second statute running against the clinician.
- Do not print “OH must assess night / lone / home working.” No FOM, SOM or ACAS product creates that named requirement. A silent report is a practice gap, not an automatic breach.
- Do not print “duty of care breach”, “non-compliant OH report”, “unlawful lone-working practice”, or “non-compliant with the Act” from this batch. Prompt 49 / house legal discipline. Need a named statute, regulation or judgment.
- Do not print INDG73 as if it forbids text or app check-in. The leaflet already lists email and “other devices designed to raise the alarm.” The finding is that it **does not name Deaf/BSL**, not that HSE banned accessible methods.
- Do not print a national rate of Deaf staff on night or lone rotas. None was found.
- Do not print a fulfilment rate for vibrating pagers. None was found.
- Do not present *Iqbal v Home Office* 2200324/2016 as a victory case, a night-shift case, or a home-working-transfer case. It is office-hours BSL hours plus an OH core-hours note. The claimant lost.
- Do not present *Sheikh v CIS Security*, the BBC late-shift EAT commentary, or *Mahoro v Northern Care Alliance* as Deaf cases. They are nearby shift / OH / lone-working judgments about other impairments.
- Do not collapse AtW into the employer’s adjustment duty. AtW funds items *beyond* what the employer is expected to provide. Cap, freeze and processing live in 52–54 — do not re-litigate them here.
- Do not treat a workplace assessment (AtW or commercial) as an FOM/SOM official OH template. No specimen Deaf OH report was found.
- Do not cite PAS 79:2007 as the live fire-risk product. Housing PAS 79-2 was withdrawn 6 August 2021.
- Do not paste MCA / seafarer Approved Doctor hearing standards into an office or care OH cell. Those are fitness-to-hear-radio-and-alarms-at-sea.
- Do not paste OSHA 1910.165, ADA §215, EEOC strobe/pager examples, or *Strudwick* (Ontario 2015) into a UK data cell. International = context only (prompt 95).
- Do not confuse **workplace PEEPs** (Fire Safety Order + 2007/2023 disabled-escape guide) with **Residential PEEPs** under the Fire Safety (Residential Evacuation Plans) (England) Regulations 2025. The 2025 regs are specified residential buildings, not the employer’s office or ward.
- Do not paste NHSScotland or Irish HSE lone-working policy into an England sentence.
- Do not use Northern Ireland as if it were on the Equality Act for employment. NI remains on the DDA 1995 (as amended).
- Do not recycle Rimmer, Clarke, Bussey, Elliott or Thomas into this batch. Wrong domains.
- Do not hold the page out as employment-law advice, or as a comment on any named person’s live OH referral.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment: who books the interpreter for the OH appointment, who pays for the pager, who owns the PEEP.

## Nice to add (found after the findings files were compiled — use, do not pretend 92–94 already had it)

These do **not** fill the national-protocol cell. They show that the *PEEP layer*, not the OH layer, is where the alone / out-of-hours question already lives.

1. **Kent County Council PEEP standard HS S 012 (15 January 2025).** Hearing-impaired options on the menu include flashing beacons, buddy system, vibrating pagers, alternative communication system. Example interview questions include “Do you ever work alone?” and “Do you have any difficulties hearing the fire alarm?” This is one large employer’s standard, not HSE. Do not upgrade it to a national mandate. Source: KELSI PDF `Personal-emergency-evacuation-plan-standard.pdf`.
2. **Off-the-shelf PEEP templates** already tick “pager / visual alarm / colleague or buddy” and, in at least one commercial template (High Speed Training), ask whether the evacuation procedure has been given in **BSL**. Again: template market, not a statute.
3. **Sector fire-safety notes** (e.g. care-provider PEEP guidance) already say deaf or hearing-impaired people **working alone** may need an alternative alert (flashing light or vibrating pager) because the informal “watch colleagues leave” method fails when there is no colleague. That sentence is the anchor scenario in plain English. It is still not an OH protocol and not a fulfilment series.
4. **SOM written evidence ESD0047 (2026)** to the Work and Pensions Committee asked for employer guidance on **home and hybrid** models for disabled workers generally (risk of “ghettoising”). Useful as a professional-body ask. Not a Deaf product.
5. **BMJ 17 September 2025** letter, Nwosu & Kaul, “Occupational health guidance for hard of hearing colleagues” (`BMJ` 2025;390:r1910). Confirms NHS occupational-medicine doctors are writing internally about this population. Full protocol was paywalled on the findings pass. Re-open the letter before quoting anything beyond the title. Not first-person employee testimony.
6. **Working Time Regulations 1998 night-worker health assessment** is a separate fitness-for-night-work offer. It is not a BSL communication assessment. Do not merge it with the OH reasonable-adjustment report.

None of these changes the headline: no FOM/SOM BSL standard, no HSE Deaf lone-working annex, no named UK night-shift Deaf judgment.
