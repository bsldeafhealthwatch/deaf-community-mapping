It is not 20–26 / 27–33 (benefits and Jobcentre), not 52–54 (AtW cap and waits as a BSL-interpreter product), not 66–71 (older-age care), not 81–83 (whether NRCPD is a statutory regulator), not 92–96 (OH legal basis and PEEP / lone working), not 97–102 (HR ticket speed), not 103–108 (recruitment-stage CSI), not 109–114 (grievance clock). ## Verdict for any public page

**Curate first. Draft almost nothing.**

The one sentence a page is allowed to lead with, if any:

> A DeafBlind employee’s workplace adjustment is not the same product as a BSL interpreter for a hearing-sighted Deaf colleague. The live NRCPD category is Registered Interpreter for Deafblind People (RIDB) — a two-digit national bench. Communicator-guide is a job, not an NRCPD category. Access to Work can fund a support worker or travel buddy and has no separate DeafBlind tariff; the cap is one number. There is no official count of DeafBlind people in work.

## Don't list (print near any in-work / adjustments page)

- Do not print current AtW funding levels, employer provision, or the absence of DeafBlind-specific guidance as unlawful discrimination or a breach of duty without a named statute, regulation, or tribunal/court judgment.
- Do not print “unregistered guide-communicator = illegal.” CG is not an NRCPD category. Absence of a category is not a criminal offence.
- Do not print **8 RIDB** as current. That is the Feb 2025 / Jun 2026 government baseline. Live table on the 115 pass was **19**. Dual registrations mean 19 is a category total.
- Do not print **68 RIDB** as delivered. That is the £15k competency-grant aspiration (23 Jun 2026).
- Do not add SRLPDC (2 Deafblind Manual + 1 Guide Communicator, accounts to 31 Mar 2025) onto the NRCPD 19.
- Do not call Deafblind Enablement’s Professional Register a national regulator. Page exists; headcount unpublished.
- Do not treat Signature Level 2/3 communicator-guide awards as a licence.
- Do not print a separate AtW “RIDB hourly band.” Staff guide interpreter card is still BSL National £26–47 / London £30–52 (no 2026 uprate found on the 116 pass). NUBSLI April 2026 has no RIDB column.
- Do not print the cap as two numbers. One ceiling: **£69,260**, frozen 8 Apr 2024 through 31 Mar 2027. Do not copy RNID’s printed £69,620.
- Do not print Sense ATW0075 (19 Jun 2014) as 2026 staff-guide practice. Named cluster: manual vs cheaper-BSL challenge, 14-week renewal gap, 15→10 hours, “fifth of going rate,” £30k salaried cap. Historic written evidence.
- Do not print cost share as applying to support workers. Live employer factsheet: 100% consider for support workers; SAE/premises take the size threshold.
- Do not print “HSE requires a workplace guide-communicator.” HSWA / MHSWR are general. HSE disability pages do not mention deafblind, guiding or workplace orientation.
- Do not print RNIB spoken hazard call-outs as a DeafBlind protocol. Wrong channel if the colleague is also Deaf.
- Do not file prompt 94 (PEEP, lone working, visual/vibrating alarm) as the answer to Tuesday-afternoon navigation.
- Do not print Norway’s uncapped NAV interpreter-guide hours, US SSP monthly caps, Australian EAF dollar caps, or NAATI RPDBI as UK rules. International (118).
- Do not print “the US has workplace SSPs so England is non-compliant.” US SSP is a state-by-state community patchwork; ADA still sits on the employer.
- Do not print a national “employers treat DeafBlind staff as Deaf” rate. Two UK sources, twelve years apart: Fletcher 2019 and Sense 2014.
- Do not reuse Francesca Bussey, Pearl Clinton, Alan Graham, Mrs W (health / EOL). Do not reuse Rimmer or Clarke (JCP). Do not reuse Tomasz Borys (deaf BSL AtW cap, not DeafBlind).
- Do not stretch Locked Out (health and social care, 27 Nov 2025) into an employer HR cell.
- Do not print the RNID/DeafATW 2026 survey (n=358) as a DeafBlind finding. Title includes deafblind; published splits and case AB are BSL.
- Do not print Sense **451,211** or **125,452 aged 20–69** as a census or Care Act register. Model: ORS 2017 on Robertson & Emerson 2010 rates. Same model underlies SWL 8,000–11,000.
- Do not print the June 2026 “**12,000** Deafblind people with communication needs” as ONS. Briefing denominator for the RIDB grant.
- Do not use 125,000 working-age modelled stock as the denominator for 19 RIDB. Different populations (later-life acquired dual sensory loss vs congenital / Usher / BSL-then-sight-loss).
- Do not print a UK DeafBlind employment rate. There isn’t one. Irish 23% / US 29% stay international.
- Do not print Census BSL-main (~22,000–25,000 E&W) as a DeafBlind count.
- Do not print AtW 3,210 BSL-interpreter customers as a DeafBlind customer count.
- Do not wear a DEI or anti-DEI banner. Follow money and fulfilment — who is on which register, what the award letter actually names, who walks the person to the third-floor meeting.
