# Team prompts inbox

A running log of research questions and prompts the team has sent Graeme, to be pasted into a Claude session when there's time to work on them. Add a new entry at the top whenever something comes in; move it to "Done" (with a one-line note on the outcome) once it's been looked into.

**How to use this:** when a teammate sends you a question or idea, add it below under "Waiting" with today's date and who it's from. When you get to it, paste the prompt text into a Claude session. Once it's answered, move the entry down to "Done" and note where the answer ended up (a dossier section, a new research-framework file, a reply to the person, etc).
