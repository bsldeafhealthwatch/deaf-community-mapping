What is not on this map — and why

No borough-by-borough figure exists for the deafblind population in South West London. The 8,000 to 11,000 range quoted elsewhere in this project is a modelled planning estimate, produced by applying Sense's national prevalence rate to each borough's population — it is not a measured or registered figure, and no NHS or council dataset specific to deafblind people in South West London was found to exist publicly as of August 2026. Plotting a false-precision figure per borough would misrepresent the evidence. See the Gap Tracker document for the full list of local data that still needs to be obtained by direct contact with each council.
